# Medieval population reference totals (not on the map)

Regional and country totals of Jewish population, number of communities and related estimates that are NOT town rows in `kehilot.csv` (the map holds only a town's own Jewish population). Collected for reference; each block names its source. Page/line references are to the working text files kept in the owner's workspace.

## Rivka Shpak-Lissak: population estimates for the Land of Israel (Jews, Samaritans, Muslims, Christians; regional, sanjak, urban/rural; 4th c. BCE - 20th c.)

See `LAND_OF_ISRAEL_POPULATION_ESTIMATES.csv` (548 lines, quoting the scholars the books cite: Broshi, Baron, Rapoport, Prawer, Beider's Ottoman census analyses and others). Sources: R. Shpak-Lissak, *When and how the Jewish majority in the Land of Israel was eliminated* (Carmel; 443 e-reader pages) and *When and how the Arabs and Muslims immigrated to the Land of Israel, 640-1914* (Yedioth Ahronoth / Sifrei Hemed; 474 e-reader pages). All figures were checked against the screenshots; several of the books' figures are contradicted by Beider (2023, Genealogy 7(3):49), see the workspace conflict files.

## Botticini, Eckstein, Vaturi: Jewish and total populations by region, province and country, 1500-1930

See `POPULATION_1500_1930_REGIONS.csv` (493 lines: Germany-Austria, Poland-Lithuania, Bohemia, Moravia, Wuerttemberg, Saxony, Baden, Bavaria, Hesse, Congress Poland, Galicia, Pale of Settlement, Posen, with the paper's table numbers and the sources it cites). Source: M. Botticini, Z. Eckstein, A. Vaturi, *The Chosen Many: Population Growth and Jewish Childcare in Central-Eastern Europe, 1500-1930*, Pinhas Sapir Center for Development, Tel Aviv University, Discussion Paper 4-16 (2016). Town series from the same paper (Prague, Vienna, Frankfurt, Wroclaw, Warsaw, Lublin, Poznan, Lodz, Kyiv ...) are rows in kehilot.csv; known errors in the paper: Wroclaw 1910 (printed 3,800; the YIVO table gives 20,212), Frankfurt 1817 (3,928 printed, 3,298 in EJ), Vienna 1670 share (20.2% printed, should be 2.0%).
