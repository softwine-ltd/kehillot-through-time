# Unavailable sources: Yavne (Jamnia), Israel

- Fischer, M. and Taxel, I., 2007. "Ancient Yavneh: Its History and Archaeology." Tel Aviv 34: 204-284. https://www.academia.edu/3218807 — Comprehensive history/archaeology of Yavneh; likely discusses Hasmonean Judaization, Early Roman Jewish town, site extent (any size/population basis). — academia.edu 403; journal paywalled (T&F).
- Ameling, W. et al. (eds.), 2014. Corpus Inscriptionum Iudaeae/Palaestinae (CIIP) III: South Coast, "Introduction to Iamnia," pp. 151-157. De Gruyter. — Scholarly summary of Iamnia's history, Jewish/gentile tensions and inscriptions. — Not online (paywalled); only cited by the EPI-identity database page.
- Kasher, A., 1990. Jews and Hellenistic Cities in Eretz-Israel (332 BCE-70 CE). TSAJ 21, Mohr Siebeck, esp. pp. 31-32, 84, 92-94, 113, 121-124. — Hasmonean conquest and Judaization of Jamnia, Jewish-gentile relations, Philo's altar episode. — Paywalled / not online.
- Schürer, E., rev. Vermes, Millar & Goodman, 1973-1979. The History of the Jewish People in the Age of Jesus Christ, vol. 1 pp. 159-165 and vol. 2 (Jamnia entry under Hellenistic cities). T&T Clark. — Standard account of Jamnia's population composition and political status. — Not accessible online (Google Books blocked).
- Shahar, Y., 2005. "Talmudic Yavneh - Two Generations, Then Eternal Glory." In M. Fischer (ed.), Yavneh, Yavneh-Yam and Their Neighborhood, Tel Aviv, pp. 113-138 (Hebrew). — Status of Yavneh at the end of the Second Temple period and its Jewish population. — Not online.
- Farhi, Y. and Bachar, R., 2020. "Iamneia (by the Sea?): A Newly Discovered Mint in First Century BCE Provincia Syria." Israel Numismatic Research 15: 111-126. https://www.lockwoodonlinejournals.com/index.php/inr/article/view/2802 — First-century BCE civic coinage of Iamneia (post-Pompeian status of the town). — Abstract only; full text paywalled.
- Safrai, Z., 2022. "The Hasmonaean Battle near Iamnia (Yavne)." In Haddad, E. et al. (eds.), Yavne and its Secrets: Collected Papers, Jerusalem (Hebrew). — Hasmonean-Seleucid warfare at Iamnia. — Not online.
- Betzer, P. and Varga, D. et al. (eds.), 2022. Yavne and its Secrets: Collected Papers. IAA, Jerusalem (Hebrew). Incl. Perry-Gal, Betzer & Varga, "Preliminary Insights on the Economy and Industry of Yavne in the Roman Period." — Recent IAA finds incl. Roman-period remains and possible Jewish material culture. — Not online.
- Ustinova, Y., Betzer, P. and Varga, D., 2023. "A New Inscribed Sling Bullet from Iamnia (Yavne)." Israel Exploration Journal 72: 189-202. — Hellenistic pagan city cults vs. Hasmonean attacks. — Paywalled.
- Kasher, A., 1992. "A Second-Century BCE Greek Inscription from Iamnia." Cathedra 63: 14-15 (Hebrew). — Sidonian/pagan character of pre-Hasmonean Iamnia. — Not online.
- Kletter, R., 2004. "Tel Yavne." Hadashot Arkheologiyot / ESI 116. — Status of Iamnia as an imperial estate after Salome (cited by Wikipedia). — Not located/fetched.
- Kletter, R., Ziffer, I. and Zwickel, W., 2010. Yavneh I. OBO SA 30. — Site history and archaeology. — Not accessed (open-access PDF not located).
- Fischer, M., Taxel, I. and Amit, D., 2008. "Rural Settlement in the Vicinity of Yavneh in the Byzantine Period: A Religio-Archaeological Perspective." BASOR 350: 7-35. — Background on Yavneh region (mostly later periods). — Paywalled (JSTOR).
- Rogers, G. M., 2021. For the Freedom of Zion. Yale UP, pp. 5, 86, 136, 281, 539. — Jamnia altar episode and Jamnia in the First Revolt (Neapolitanus meeting Agrippa II, 66 CE). — Not online.
