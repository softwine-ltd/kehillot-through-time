# Unavailable sources: Ein Gedi, Israel (Second Temple period)

- Hirschfeld, Y. (n.d., Hecht Museum catalogue). "Ein Gedi: A Large Jewish Village." Hecht Museum, University of Haifa. https://mushecht2.haifa.ac.il/images/catalogues/archeology/Hirschpeld_Eng.pdf — Search snippets indicate a pottery/coin table for the Second Temple (Early Roman) stratum (24% of pottery, 35 Hasmonean coins) and a population estimate of 875+ at 25 persons/dunam for the village "at the apex of its development" (apparently the Byzantine village). — Server returned 503 to WebFetch (twice) and an empty reply to curl.
- Hirschfeld, Y. (ed.), 2007. En-Gedi Excavations II: Final Report (1996-2002). Israel Exploration Society, Jerusalem. — Full stratigraphy and finds of the Early Roman (Second Temple) village, miqveh, Hasmonean coins, 68 CE destruction layer. — Not online (book).
- Stern, E. (ed.), 2007. En-Gedi Excavations I: Final Report (1961-1965), esp. S. Matskevich & E. Stern, "Stratum IV - The Persian Period," pp. 193-270, and chapters on Strata III-II. Israel Exploration Society. — Persian-period Yehud settlement, Hasmonean and Herodian fortress strata, possible area/size data. — Not online; only a short review (Abstracta Iranica) read.
- Hadas, G., 1994. "Nine Tombs of the Second Temple Period at 'En Gedi." 'Atiqot 24: 1-14. JSTOR 23463739. — Second Temple tombs, wooden coffins, tenant-farmer interpretation of late Hasmonean Ein Gedi. — JSTOR paywall; known only via Wikipedia citation.
- Hadas, G., 2005. "Excavations at the Village of 'En Gedi: 1993-1995." 'Atiqot 49. — Village excavations; Wikipedia cites it for a c. 1,000 population estimate (Byzantine period). — Not accessed (not found open online).
- Hadas, G. & Peleg-Barkat, O., 2023. "Avigad's Excavations of the Nahal David Burial Caves and the Settlement of 'En Gedi under the Hasmoneans and Herod the Great." In New Studies in the Archaeology of the Judean Desert (IAA), pp. 247-266. — Directly on the Hasmonean/Herodian settlement and its tombs; may contain size/population data. — Only the bibliographic record (no abstract) was available.
- Avigad, N., 1962. "Expedition A - The Burial Caves in Nahal David." Israel Exploration Journal 12: 169-183. — Excavation of Second Temple-period burial caves of the Ein Gedi community. — JSTOR/IEJ paywall; not online.
- Mazar, B., Dothan, T. & Dunayevsky, I., 1966. "En-Gedi: First and Second Seasons of Excavations 1961-1962." 'Atiqot 5. — Original excavators' account of Persian, Hasmonean and Herodian strata and the royal-estate/tenant-farmer theory. — Not online (cited in Cotton 2001).
- Hirschfeld, Y., 2006. "Ramat Hanadiv and Ein Gedi: Property versus Poverty in Judea before 70." In J. H. Charlesworth (ed.), Jesus and Archaeology, Eerdmans, pp. 384-392. — Pre-70 Ein Gedi village character, likely size discussion. — Book, not online (Google Books blocked).
- Hirschfeld, Y., 2004. "A Community of Hermits above En-Gedi" / "Ein Gedi: 'A Very Large Village of Jews'" (Cathedra 96, 2000; and related publications). — Essene/hermit site and village size. — Not online / Hebrew journal paywall.
- Amit, D. & Magness, J., 2000. "The Essenes did not Live above Ein Gedi: A Reply to Y. Hirschfeld." Cathedra 96: 57ff. (Hebrew). — Critique of the Essene site interpretation. — Not online.
- Porath, Y., 2021. The Synagogue at En-Gedi (Qedem 64). — Cited by Wikipedia for the Herodian-period greatest extent of the settlement. — Not online.
- Mashiach, A. & Davidovich, U., 2024. "Revisiting Late Iron Age En-Gedi: A Stratigraphic Reassessment of Stratum V at Tel Goren." BASOR 392: 179-205. — Foundation and early-6th-century destruction of the Judahite town (Year Established). — Paywalled; abstract only.
- Farhi, Y., 2022. "A Ring from a Cave in 'En Gedi and the Conflict Between Herod the Great and Mattathias Antigonus (40-37 BCE)." Jerusalem Journal of Archaeology 3: 3-13. — Dating/identity of Nahal David burials. — Only abstract page read (full text not fetched).
- Cotton, H. M., 2022. "Ein Gedi between the Two Revolts." In Roman Rule and Jewish Life, De Gruyter. — Revised version of the SCI 2001 article (the 2001 version was read). — De Gruyter, not accessed.
