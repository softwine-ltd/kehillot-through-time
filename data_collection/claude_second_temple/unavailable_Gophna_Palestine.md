- Zelinger, Yehiel, 2001. "Evidence for a Jewish settlement in Gophna in the Second Temple period and the Bar Kokhba War" (Hebrew; English gloss of title). Cited by Wikipedia "Jifna". — Excavation of the 1995 Jifna burial cave (Hasmonean-early Roman pottery); likely the key archaeological report on Second Temple Gophna, possibly with settlement extent. — Not online; only cited.
- Klein, Eitan, 2011. "Gophna during the Late Roman Period in Light of Artistic and Epigraphic Finds." In Tavger, Amar & Billig (eds.), In the Highland's Depth, pp. 119-134 (Hebrew). — Discusses end of Jewish settlement and earlier Jewish phase. — Not online; only cited.
- Lupu, E., Heimbach, M., Schneider, N. & Cotton, H. (eds.), 2018. Corpus Inscriptionum Iudaeae/Palaestinae, Vol. IV: Iudaea/Idumaea, pp. 53-58 (Gophna). De Gruyter. — Full commentary and dating of Gophna ossuaries/sarcophagi with Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek inscriptions. — Paywalled (only database summaries read).
- Van der Brink, E. & Yannai, E. (eds.), 2002. In Quest of Ancient Settlements and Landscapes: Archaeological Studies in Honour of Ram Gophna. Tel Aviv: Ramot; incl. Wolff, S.R., "Gophna, For Gophna." — Publishes Gophna ossuaries (Yo'ezer son of Yehohanan the scribe; Salome Iakimos) and site discussion. — Not online.
- Sukenik, E.L., 1933. "An Ancient Jewish Cave on the Jerusalem-Shechem Road." Bulletin of the Jewish Palestine Exploration Society 1, pp. 7-9. — Source of the 'Bilga' priestly ossuary (63 BCE-70 CE) attributed to Gophna; would confirm the findspot. — Not online.
- Lagrange, M.-J., 1895. "Chronique de Jérusalem." Revue Biblique 4.1, pp. 88-96 (JSTOR 44100081). — Original publication of the 'Yehuda son of El'azar' inscription from Gophna. — JSTOR paywall.
- Grey, Matthew J., 2011. Jewish Priests and the Social History of post-70 Palestine (PhD diss., UNC), p. 159. — Gophna's priestly Jewish population; mostly post-70 but discusses pre-70 background. — Not accessed (only cited by Wikipedia).
- Benoit, P., 1961. "Textes grecs et latins." Discoveries in the Judaean Desert II (Murabba'at), pp. 209-280. — Greek marriage contract of 124 CE naming Gophna (post-period, last evidence of Jewish presence). — Not online.
- Jones, A.H.M., 1931. "The Urbanization of Palestine." Journal of Roman Studies 21. — Toparchy system incl. Gophna. — Cambridge/JSTOR paywall (abstract only).
- Studium Biblicum Franciscanum, 2001. "Discussion Mount Ephraim and Benjamin: 51. Gophna (Jifna)." — Site history and archaeology summary; cited by Wikipedia. — Not located online.
