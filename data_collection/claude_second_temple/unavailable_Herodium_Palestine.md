# Unavailable sources: Herodium, Palestine

- Grossberg, A., Porat, R., Amit, D. and Netzer, E., 2011. "Unique Ritual Baths at Herodium from the Time of the Uprisings Against Rome." Judea and Samaria Research Studies 20, pp. 63-80 (Hebrew). — Why relevant: the full study of the two miqvaot by the rebels' synagogue, their dating (First vs. Second Revolt) and a possible Idumean custom. — Why not accessed: not online, known only from citations on herodium.org and in Schwartz 2015.
- Grossberg, A., Porat, R., Amit, D. and Netzer, E., 2011. "Miqva'ot from the anti-Roman rebellions built in the style of 'one miqveh on top of another' from Herodium." Tehumin 31, pp. 395-440 (Hebrew). — Why relevant: the same miqvaot, with halakhic analysis. — Why not accessed: not online.
- Eshel, E., 2015. "The Hebrew and Aramaic Inscriptions from the Area of the Tomb at Herodium." In R. Porat, R. Chachy and Y. Kalman, Herodium: Final Reports of the 1972-2010 Excavations I, Jerusalem: IES, pp. 460-473. https://lisa.biu.ac.il/sites/lisa/files/shared/herodium_i.pdf — Why relevant: ostraca with Jewish names, 1st c. BCE to 1st c. CE, some from the refuse dump of the First Revolt. — Why not accessed: the PDF returned 503 to WebFetch and connection reset to curl; the catalogue page shows metadata only.
- Eshel, E., Misgav, H. and Porat, R., c. 2023. "Legal Ostraca from Herodium." Scripta Classica Israelica (per search snippets). — Why relevant: legal documents of the Great Revolt period, possibly brought by refugees. — Why not accessed: abstract only (cited from the abstract page).
- [Coin report on the 1968-1969 Foerster excavations at Herodium], Israel Numismatic Research. https://lockwoodonlinejournals.com/index.php/inr/article/view/2745 — Why relevant: counts of First Revolt coins and the extent of rebel habitation. — Why not accessed: abstract only; full text paywalled.
- Porat, R., Kalman, Y. and Chachy, R., 2015. "Synthesis and Archaeological-Historical Discussion." Herodium Final Reports I, pp. 515-534. — Why relevant: the excavators' synthesis of the Herodian and rebel phases. — Why not accessed: not online (only individual chapters are posted on herodium.org).
- Corbo, V., 1989. Herodion I: Gli edifici della reggia-fortezza. Jerusalem: Franciscan Printing Press. — Why relevant: the final report on the Palace-Fortress (synagogue conversion, which per Flesher 1998 the final report assigns to the Second Revolt; 41 First Revolt coins). — Why not accessed: not online.
- Netzer, E., 1981. Greater Herodium (Qedem 13). Jerusalem: Hebrew University. — Why relevant: Lower Herodium layout and size, Herodian occupation. — Why not accessed: not online.
- Netzer, E., 2005. "New Discoveries at the Excavations of Lower Herodium." Qadmoniot (Hebrew). — Why relevant: Lower Herodium and its ritual baths. — Why not accessed: not online.
- Kasher, A., 1988. Jews, Idumaeans, and Ancient Arabs (TSAJ 18). Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, pp. 224-239, 232-233 n. 70. — Why relevant: argues that Idumean Jews held Herodium in the First Revolt. — Why not accessed: paywalled / Google Books.
- Stern, M., 1974. Greek and Latin Authors on Jews and Judaism I, pp. 474-478 (Pliny, NH 5.70, toparchies). — Why relevant: Herodium as a toparchy capital in Pliny's list. — Why not accessed: not online.
- Hengel, M., 1989. The Zealots, p. 367. — Why relevant: an early rebel takeover of the fortresses, including Herodium. — Why not accessed: not online.
- Reich, R., 2013. Miqwa'ot (Jewish Ritual Baths) in the Second Temple, Mishnaic and Talmudic Periods. Jerusalem: Yad Ben-Zvi and IES (Hebrew). — Why relevant: catalogue and dating of the Herodium miqvaot; double-pool interpretation disputed. — Why not accessed: not online (JRA review paywalled).
