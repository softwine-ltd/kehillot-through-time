# Unavailable sources: Delos, Greece

- Bordreuil, P. and Bruneau, Ph., 1982. "Les Israélites de Délos et la juiverie délienne." Bulletin de Correspondance Hellénique 106, 465-504. https://doi.org/10.3406/bch.1982.1925 — Editio princeps of the two Samaritan "Israelites" stelae with their dating, plus Bruneau's full discussion of the Delian Jewish colony, GD 80 and the Rheneia imprecations. — Only the Persée landing page with the opening paragraph was readable; the PDF download returned an HTML page.
- Trümper, M., 2004. "The Oldest Original Synagogue Building in the Diaspora: The Delos Synagogue Reconsidered." Hesperia 73.4, 513-598. https://www.jstor.org/stable/4134903 — Phase-by-phase architectural dating of GD 80 (2nd c. BCE to 2nd c. CE) and arguments for synagogue use. — Abstract only; full text on JSTOR (paywall).
- Trümper, M., 2020. "The Synagogue in Delos Revisited." In Synagogues in the Hellenistic-Roman Period (volume cited by Wikipedia search result). — Updated discussion of GD 80 and the Jewish community. — Not accessed (not online / paywall).
- White, L. M., 1987. "The Delos Synagogue Revisited: Recent Fieldwork in the Graeco-Roman Diaspora." Harvard Theological Review 80.2, 133-160. — Re-examination of the Samaritan inscriptions; argues for Jewish and Samaritan communities and a Samaritan synagogue. — Cambridge abstract page only (paywall).
- Runesson, A., 1999. "The Oldest Original Synagogue Building in the Diaspora: A Response to L. Michael White." Harvard Theological Review 92. — GD 80 debate. — Paywall.
- Matassa, L., 2007. "Unravelling the Myth of the Synagogue on Delos." Bulletin of the Anglo-Israel Archaeological Society 25. https://www.academia.edu/233507 — Critique of the synagogue identification (superseded by her 2018 book, which was read). — Not fetched (academia.edu download blocked).
- Plassart, A., 1913/1914. "La synagogue juive de Délos." Mélanges Holleaux 201-215; Revue Biblique 23, 523-534. — Original identification of GD 80 and the Theos Hypsistos inscriptions. — Not online.
- Mazur, B. D., 1935. Studies on Jewry in Greece. Athens: Hestia. — First rebuttal of the synagogue identification; Jewish presence on Delos. — Not online.
- Binder, D. D., 1999. Into the Temple Courts: The Place of the Synagogues in the Second Temple Period. SBL. — Occupation scenarios for GD 80 after 88/69 BCE. — Not online (cited via Matassa).
- Levine, L. I., 2005. The Ancient Synagogue: The First Thousand Years, 2nd ed., Yale, pp. 100-103. — Delos synagogue and Jewish/Samaritan communities. — Not online.
- Saar, O.-P., 2024. "Cursing Beyond the Grave: Imprecations and Jewish Funerary Culture in Antiquity." Entangled Religions. https://er.ceres.rub.de/index.php/ER/article/view/11576 — Discussion and dating of the Rheneia imprecations. — Blocked by bot protection (Anubis access denied).
- Salvo, I., "Sweet Revenge. Emotional Factors in Prayers for Justice," pp. 237-244. — Analysis of the Rheneia imprecations. — Citation only.
- van der Horst, P. W., 1991. Ancient Jewish Epitaphs, p. 149; van der Horst, P. W. and Newman, J. H., 2008. Early Jewish Prayers in Greek (De Gruyter). — Translation and commentary on the Rheneia prayer for vengeance. — Not online.
- Noy, D., Panayotov, A. and Bloedhorn, H., 2004. Inscriptiones Judaicae Orientis I: Eastern Europe (Mohr Siebeck), Delos/Rheneia entries. — Full corpus of Jewish and Samaritan inscriptions from Delos with dates. — Not online.
- Kraabel, A. T., 1984. "New Evidence of the Samaritan Diaspora Has Been Found on Delos." Biblical Archaeologist 47. — Samaritan inscriptions. — Not accessed (paywall).
- Pucci Ben Zeev, M., 1998. Jewish Rights in the Roman World (Mohr Siebeck), on AJ 14.213-216 and 14.231-232. — Dating and authenticity of the Josephus documents about the Jews of Delos. — Not online.
- Kotansky, R., 2020. Article in Greek, Roman, and Byzantine Studies citing the Rheneia tablets. https://grbs.library.duke.edu/index.php/grbs/article/download/16357/7282/20518 — Possibly further discussion of the Rheneia imprecations. — Not fetched (found late; secondary to the question).
