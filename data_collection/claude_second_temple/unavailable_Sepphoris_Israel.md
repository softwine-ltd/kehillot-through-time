# Unavailable sources: Sepphoris, Israel

- Schumer, Nathan, 2017. "The Population of Sepphoris: Rethinking Urbanization in Early and Middle Roman Galilee." Journal of Ancient Judaism 8.1, 90-111. DOI 10.13109/jaju.2017.8.1.90. — Archaeological demographic model giving early Roman Sepphoris 2,000-4,300 people (about half earlier estimates); key modern estimate. — academia.edu 403, relbib browser check, publisher page 403; figure seen only in search snippets.
- Chancey, Mark A. and Eric M. Meyers, 2000. "How Jewish Was Sepphoris in Jesus' Time?" Biblical Archaeology Review 26:4, 18-33, 61. https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/article/how-jewish-was-sepphoris-in-jesus-time/ — Full discussion of mikvaot, stone vessels, pig bones, Hasmonean coins and ostracon; Jewish share of first-century population. — Paywall (only excerpt and captions read).
- Reed, Jonathan L., 2000. Archaeology and the Galilean Jesus: A Re-examination of the Evidence. Harrisburg: Trinity Press International, esp. pp. 49-51, 117. — Area x density population estimate for Sepphoris (often cited as 8,000-12,000) and Jewish ethnic markers. — Book not online.
- Reed, Jonathan L., 1994. "Population Numbers, Urbanization, and Economics: Galilean Archaeology and the Historical Jesus." SBL 1994 Seminar Papers, 203-219. — Original population calculation for Sepphoris. — Only catalogue record online.
- Meyers, Eric M., 1997. "Jesus and His Galilean Context." In Edwards and McCollough (eds.), Archaeology and the Galilee, 57-66 (p. 59). — Estimate of 18,000 for Sepphoris. — Not online (figure seen via Fortress Press introduction).
- Overman, J. Andrew, 1988. "Who Were the First Urban Christians?" SBL 1988 Seminar Papers, 160-168. — Estimate of 30,000 for Sepphoris. — Not online (seen via citation).
- Horsley, Richard A., 1996. Archaeology, History, and Society in Galilee. Valley Forge: Trinity Press International, p. 45. — 15,000 for Sepphoris and Tiberias combined. — Not online (seen via citation).
- Naveh, Joseph, 2002. "Epigraphic Miscellanea." Israel Exploration Journal 52.2, 240-253. — Edition and reading of the 2nd c. BCE Hebrew ostracon/jar inscription from Sepphoris (SEPP0100). — Paywall/not online.
- Meyers, Eric M., Ehud Netzer and Carol L. Meyers, 1992. Sepphoris. Winona Lake: Eisenbrauns. — Persian and Hellenistic finds, Hasmonean coins, statement that Jewish population goes back at least to Hellenistic times; priests from Sepphoris. — Book not online (quoted excerpts only).
- Meyers, Eric M., 2009. "The Persian Period at Sepphoris." Eretz-Israel 29. — Whether the Persian-period settlement/garrison had Judahite residents. — Not online.
- Weiss, Zeev and Ehud Netzer, 1996. "Hellenistic and Roman Sepphoris: The Archaeological Evidence." In Nagy et al. (eds.), Sepphoris in Galilee: Crosscurrents of Culture, 29-37. — Hellenistic/Hasmonean occupation of the acropolis, early Roman city. — Not online (quoted excerpts only); Weiss project site returned 503.
- Strange, James F., 1992. "Six Campaigns at Sepphoris: The University of South Florida Excavations, 1983-1989." In Levine (ed.), The Galilee in Late Antiquity, 339-356. — Stratigraphy of Hasmonean and early Roman remains. — Not online.
- Eshel, Hanan, 2000. "We Need More Data" / "They're Not Ritual Baths." Biblical Archaeology Review 26:4. — Counter-argument on the stepped pools; states the Jewish percentage of first-century population is unknowable. — Paywall.
- Grantham, William, 1996. "A Zooarchaeological Model for the Study of Ethnic Complexity at Sepphoris." PhD diss., Northwestern University. — Pig-bone evidence for Jewish population by period. — Not online.
- Miller, Stuart S., 2015. At the Intersection of Texts and Material Finds: Stepped Pools, Stone Vessels, and Ritual Purity among the Jews of Roman Galilee. Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht. — Dating of Sepphoris stepped pools. — Not online.
- Sherman, Maya, Zeev Weiss, Tami Zilberman and Gal Yasur, 2020. "Chalkstone Vessels from Sepphoris: Galilean Production in Roman Times." BASOR 383, 79-95. DOI 10.1086/707583. — Quantities and stratigraphic dates of early Roman stone vessels. — Paywall (abstract only).
- Chancey, Mark A., 2002. The Myth of a Gentile Galilee. SNTSMS 118. Cambridge University Press, pp. 79-90. — Detailed evidence for Jewish character of first-century Sepphoris and the 8,000-12,000 estimate. — Paywall/Google Books.
- Batey, Richard A., 1991. Jesus and the Forgotten City. Grand Rapids: Baker; and 2001, "Sepphoris and the Jesus Movement," New Testament Studies 47. — Population and urban character of Antipas' Sepphoris. — Not online / paywall.
- Casey, Maurice, 2010. Jesus of Nazareth. T&T Clark, pp. 163-167. — Describes first-century Sepphoris as "a very Jewish city". — Not online (seen via Wikipedia).
