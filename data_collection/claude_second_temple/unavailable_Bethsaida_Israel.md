# Unavailable sources: Bethsaida, Israel

- Arav, R. and Freund, R. A. (eds.), 1995-2009. *Bethsaida: A City by the North Shore of the Sea of Galilee*, 4 vols. Kirksville, MO: Thomas Jefferson University Press. — Final reports for et-Tell: Hellenistic/Early Roman strata, stone vessels, Herodian lamps, coins (Hasmonean, Philip), site size. Any scholarly population estimate would most likely be here. — Not online (print monograph).
- Freund, R. A., 1995. "The Search for Bethsaida in Rabbinic Literature." In Arav & Freund (eds.), *Bethsaida* vol. 1, pp. 267-311. — Rabbinic references to Tzaidan (1st-3rd c. CE) on fishing and Jewish residents. — Not online.
- Notley, R. S., 2007. "Et-Tell is Not Bethsaida." *Near Eastern Archaeology* 70: 220-230. — Analysis of the meagre Early Roman remains at et-Tell (seen only in quotations). — Paywall (JSTOR/UChicago).
- Arav, R., 2011. "Bethsaida - A Response to Steven Notley." *Near Eastern Archaeology* 74: 92-100. — Herodian lamps and limestone vessels at et-Tell, the Julias temple, Roman-period growth (seen only in quotations). — Paywall.
- Arav, R. and Savage, C. E., 2015. "Bethsaida." In D. A. Fiensy and J. R. Strange (eds.), *Galilee in the Late Second Temple and Mishnaic Periods*, vol. 2. Fortress Press. — Summary of Hellenistic-Early Roman Bethsaida; may give site size or population. — Not online (Google Books preview blocked).
- Savage, C. E., 2011. *Biblical Bethsaida: A Study of the First Century CE in the Galilee*. Lexington Books. — Social reconstruction of 1st-century Bethsaida from the et-Tell data; possible household/population discussion. — Not online (only catalogue records seen).
- Aviam, M. and Notley, R. S., 2021. "In Search of the City of the Apostles." *Novum Testamentum* 63: 143-158. — Peer-reviewed el-Araj report: Early Roman Jewish finds and urban phase. — Paywall (Brill); only bibliographic records seen.
- Kindler, A., 1989. "The Coins of the Tetrarch Philip and Bethsaida." *Cathedra* 53 (Hebrew). — Dates Julias foundation coins (30 CE). — Not online.
- Urman, D. and Flesher, P. V. M. (eds.), 1995. *Ancient Synagogues: Historical Analysis and Archaeological Discovery*, vol. 2, pp. 522-523. Brill. — Survey of el-Araj: coins of Philip (29/33 CE) and Agrippa II; synagogue-type architecture. — Not accessed (Brill / Google Books); cited by Aviam & Notley 2017.
- Kuhn, H.-W. and Arav, R., 2015. *Betsaida/Bethsaida-Julias (et-Tell): Die ersten 25 Jahre der Ausgrabung 1987-2011*. Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht. — Overview of et-Tell coins, Herodian lamps and stone vessels. — Not online (bookseller listing only).
- Syon, D., Aviam, M., Notley, R. S. and Kohn-Tavor, A. "A Tri-Metal Second-Century-CE Coin Hoard from el-A'araj-Bethsaida." *Israel Numismatic Research* (lockwoodonlinejournals.com/index.php/inr/article/view/2913). — Coin hoard and numismatic context at el-Araj (mostly after 74 CE). — Abstract only (paywall).
- Nun, M., 1998. "Has Bethsaida Finally Been Found?" *Jerusalem Perspective* 54: 12-31. — Argues et-Tell's houses are not fishermen's houses and el-Araj is the lakeside village. — Not accessed (subscription).
- Stepansky, Y., 1991. "Kefar Nahum Map, Survey." *Excavations and Surveys in Israel* 10: 87-90. — Early Roman pottery at el-Araj. — Not online.
- Israel Ministry of Foreign Affairs, "Bethsaida - An Ancient Fishing Village on the shore of the Sea of Galilee" (embassies.gov.il), and the University of Nebraska Omaha Bethsaida project history page. — Reportedly describe the Hasmonean conquest and abandonment c. 100 BCE and Jewish resettlement under Herod (Herodian coins, lamps, limestone vessels). — HTTP 503 on fetch.
