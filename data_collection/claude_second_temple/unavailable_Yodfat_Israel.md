- Adan-Bayewitz, D. and Aviam, M., 1997. "Iotapata, Josephus, and the Siege of 67: Preliminary Report on the 1992-94 Seasons." Journal of Roman Archaeology 10, 131-165. — Primary excavation report; Hasmonean-era Judaization, stratigraphy, siege evidence, possibly population reasoning. — Not online (paywalled; only bibliographic records found).
- Aviam, M., 1999. "Yodfat - Unearthing a Second-Temple and Great Revolt era Jewish city in the Galilee" (Hebrew). Qadmoniot 32, 92-101. — Hebrew excavation summary; chronology, area, possibly population estimate. — Not online.
- Aviam, M., 2002. "Yodefat/Jotapata: The Archaeology of the First Battle." In A. M. Berlin and J. A. Overman (eds.), The First Jewish Revolt: Archaeology, History, and Ideology, Routledge, 121-133. — Source of the c. 7,000 (with refugees) estimate on the eve of the siege (p. 131, per Wikipedia). — Paywalled book chapter (Routledge/Google Books).
- Aviam, M., 2007. "Yodefat and Gamla." In J. Pastor, P. Stern and M. Mor (eds.), Flavius Josephus: Interpretation and History, Brill, 372-384. — Population on eve of siege (7,000) and casualty evidence ("hundreds, possibly thousands" killed). — Paywalled (Brill); academia.edu page 403.
- Aviam, M., 2015. "Yodefat-Jotapata: A Jewish Galilean Town at the End of the Second Temple Period: The Results of an Archaeological Project." In D. A. Fiensy and J. R. Strange (eds.), Galilee in the Late Second Temple and Mishnaic Periods, vol. 2, Fortress Press, 109-126. — Latest synthesis of the excavations, Hellenistic gentile layer and Hasmonean settlement, likely population estimate. — Not online (only chapter 1 of the volume is freely available).
- Aviam, M., 2004. Jews, Pagans and Christians in the Galilee (Land of Galilee 1), University of Rochester Press, esp. 59-88. — Hasmonean conquest and Judaization of Galilee including Yodefat. — Not online.
- Rogers, G. M., 2022. For the Freedom of Zion: The Great Revolt of Jews against Romans, 66-74 CE. Yale University Press, p. 229. — Modern estimates of 7,000 on eve of siege and c. 2,000 killed at Yodefat (per Wikipedia). — Not online (paywalled book).
- Yodfat site marker (Hebrew/English historical marker), hmdb.org m=132004. — Per search snippet states population of about 4,500 and area of about 47 dunams for the Jewish town; could not verify. — 403 Forbidden.
- Israel Ministry of Foreign Affairs, "Yodefat - A Town in Galilee." — Summary of excavations (Hasmonean wall, mikvaot, 13 acres). — 503 Service Unavailable.
- Syon, D., 2015. Small Change in Hellenistic-Roman Galilee, Israel Numismatic Society, 161-166. — Hasmonean coins as evidence of Jewish settlement, likely including Yodefat coin finds. — Not online.
