- Atkinson, Kenneth, 2002. "Noble Deaths at Gamla and Masada? A Critical Assessment of Josephus' Accounts." In Berlin & Overman (eds.), The First Jewish Revolt, 349-371. — Assessment of Josephus' 9,000 figure as exaggerated. — Book, not online.

- Syon, Danny & Yavor, Zvi, 2010. Gamla II: The Architecture. The Shmarya Gutmann Excavations 1976-1989 (IAA Reports 44). — Town plan, houses, built-up area; basis for any density-based population estimate. — Not online.

- Syon, Danny, 2004. Tyre and Gamla: A Study in the Monetary Influence of Southern Phoenicia on Galilee and the Golan in the Hellenistic and Roman Periods. PhD thesis, Hebrew University of Jerusalem. — Numismatic evidence for start of Jewish settlement (pre-Jannaeus). — Not online.

- Aviam, Mordechai, 2007. "The Archaeological Illumination of Josephus' Narrative of the Battles at Yodefat and Gamla." In Z. Rodgers (ed.), Making History (Brill). — Siege, refugees, numbers. — Brill paywall.
