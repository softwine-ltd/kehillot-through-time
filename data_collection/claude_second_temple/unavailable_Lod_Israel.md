# Sources not accessed: Lod (Lydda), Israel, Second Temple period

- Schwartz, J. J., 1991. "Lod (Lydda), Israel: From its Origins through the Byzantine Period, 5600 B.C.E.-640 C.E." BAR International Series 571. Oxford: Tempus Reparatum. — The standard monograph on ancient Lod; likely holds the fullest treatment of the Persian-Hasmonean-Early Roman town, its extent and Jewish character (pp. 49-66 cited by Etya 2024 for the Hasmonean period). — Not online (book; only catalogue/review entries found).
- Raviv, D., 2019. "Granting of the Toparchies of Ephraim, Ramathaim and Lod to Hasmonean Judea." Tel Aviv 46(2): 267-285. DOI 10.1080/03344355.2019.1650500. — Archaeological/survey evidence that the Lod toparchy was Jewish-majority before the Hasmonean revolt. — Abstract only (Taylor & Francis paywall).
- Tavger, A., 2022. Article on Lod, Hadid and Ono in the Persian period (returnee list vs. archaeology). Journal of the History, Archaeology and Heritage of Lod 8. — Persian-period Benjaminite settlement at Lod; basis for the earliest Judean presence. — academia.edu page returned 403; only search snippets seen.
- Safrai, Z., 2024. "Lod as the Capital of a Toparchy." Lod "Diospolis - City of God" (Journal of the History, Archaeology and Heritage of Lod) 10. — Hasmonean/Herodian administrative role and possibly size of Lod. — Only listed in the volume's table of contents; text not found online.
- Ashkenazi, H., Gadot, Y., Shavit, A. and Shmueli, O., 2016. "Lod: settlement distribution from the Neolithic period to the present" (Hebrew). Lod "Diospolis - City of God" 2: 11-47 (fig. 9). — Reconstruction of the built-up area of Lod in the Hellenistic and Roman periods (used by Rosenfeld 2024 for area-based population estimates). — Not found online.
- Rosenberger, A. and Shavit, A., 1994. "Lod, Neve Yaraq." Hadashot Arkheologiyot 100: 46-48 (Hebrew). — Excavation of Stratum 7 at Lod attributed to the time of Alexander Jannaeus. — Not online (cited in Etya 2024).
- Zelinger, Y., 2010. "The Rural Settlement in the Lod Shephelah in the Second Temple Period." PhD dissertation, Bar-Ilan University (Hebrew). — Settlement pattern and character of the population in the Lod plain in the Second Temple period. — Not online.
- Etya, A., in preparation. "The Hasmonean and Early Roman periods in the Lod valley: a view from Tel Hadid" (Hebrew). — Regional Hasmonean/Early Roman material culture. — Not yet published.
- Rosenfeld, B.-Z., 1997. "Lod and Its Sages in the Period of the Mishnah and the Talmud" (Hebrew; Jerusalem: Yad Ben-Zvi). — Background on pre-70 Lod and population. — Not online.
- Cotton, H. M. et al. (eds.), 2018. Corpus Inscriptionum Iudaeae/Palaestinae, vol. IV (Iudaea/Idumaea), pp. 77-85 (Lydda/Diospolis introduction). De Gruyter. — Historical introduction and any early inscriptions from Lod (cited by Wikipedia for the 66 CE destruction). — Not accessed (paywalled; cited only).
- Rogers, G. M., 2021. "For the Freedom of Zion: The Great Revolt of Jews against Romans, 66-74 CE." Yale University Press, p. 169. — Cestius's march through Lydda in 66 CE (cited by Wikipedia). — Not online (book).
