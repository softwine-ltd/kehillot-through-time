- Leon, Harry J., 1960 (rev. 1995). *The Jews of Ancient Rome.* Philadelphia: Jewish Publication Society. — Standard monograph; synagogue count (11 congregations, p. 136), Leon's 1st-c. BCE dating of the Monteverde catacomb, and discussion of community size. — Not online (book; only cited via Eyland and the International Catacomb Society).
- Barclay, John M. G., 1996. *Jews in the Mediterranean Diaspora from Alexander to Trajan (323 BCE-117 CE).* Edinburgh: T&T Clark, p. 295 n. 32. — Cites three scholarly estimates of the Jews of Rome ranging from 20,000 to 60,000 (per Eyland). — Not online (book).
- Hausrath, Adolf. *Neutestamentliche Zeitgeschichte*, vol. III, p. 384. — Estimate of 40,000 Jews in Rome under Augustus and 60,000 under Tiberius (cited by Schaff). — Not checked in the original (19th-c. German book, not located online).
- Renan, Friedländer and Harnack (as cited by Schaff, History of the Christian Church I). — Estimate of 20,000-30,000 Jews in Rome in the apostolic age. — Originals not checked.
- Rocca, Samuele, 2014. "In the Beginning: The Jews as a Minority Group in the Middle and the Late Republican Period." *Scripta Judaica Cracoviensia* 12. https://ejournals.eu/czasopismo/scripta-judaica-cracoviensia/artykul/in-the-beginning-the-jews-as-a-minority-group-in-the-middle-and-the-late-republican-period — Dating of first Jewish presence (no earlier than 2nd half of 2nd c. BCE), Pompey's captives, Philo; possibly size estimates. — HTTP 503 on both the article page and the PDF download.
- Cappelletti, Silvia, 2006. *The Jewish Community of Rome: From the Second Century B.C. to the Third Century C.E.* Supplements to JSJ 113. Leiden: Brill. — Comprehensive treatment of the community's development under Tiberius, Claudius, Vespasian; likely discusses numbers. — Paywalled / not online.
- Wendt, Heidi, 2015. "Iudaica Romana: A Rereading of Judean Expulsions from Rome." *Journal of Ancient Judaism* 6: 97-126. — Reassessment of the 139 BCE, 19 CE and Claudian expulsions. — Paywalled (cited by Harland).
- Rutgers, Leonard V., 1995. *The Jews in Late Ancient Rome.* Leiden: Brill. — Catacomb-based estimate (1-2% of city population) for 3rd-4th c. CE, i.e. outside the period; also on Monteverde dating. — Not online (book).
- Juster, Jean, 1914. *Les Juifs dans l'Empire romain.* Paris. — Classic population estimates for Diaspora communities including Rome. — Not accessed (not located in a fetchable form).
- Gruen, Erich S., 2002. *Diaspora: Jews amidst Greeks and Romans.* Harvard UP, ch. on Rome. — Discussion of expulsions and of the size of the Roman community. — Not online (book).
- Smallwood, E. Mary, 1976. *The Jews under Roman Rule from Pompey to Diocletian.* Leiden: Brill. — Proposes two Claudian measures (41 and 49 CE); discussion of numbers. — Not online (cited by Rutgers).
- Bible Odyssey, "Ask a Scholar: Why did Claudius prohibit Roman Jews from meeting in synagogues in 41 C.E.?" https://www.bibleodyssey.org/ask-a-scholar/jews-in-rome/ — Dating of Claudius' 41 CE ban and later expulsion. — HTTP 403.
- Brettman, Estelle Shohet (ed. Hirschfeld, Wolsky, Dello Russo). *Vaults of Memory: The Roman Jewish Catacombs.* International Catacomb Society. — Monteverde dating debate (only an excerpt read). — Full work not accessed.
