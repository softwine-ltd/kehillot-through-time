- Applebaum, S., 1979. "Jews and Greeks in Ancient Cyrene." Studies in Judaism in Late Antiquity 28, Leiden: Brill (revised from the Hebrew edition, Jerusalem: Bialik Institute, 1969). https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/000094018 — The standard monograph on the Jews of Cyrene from Ptolemy I to 117 CE: Jewish civic status, ephebic and nomophylax inscriptions, Lucullus episode, Jonathan revolt; may hold any estimate of community size. — Not online (only catalogue and bibliography records).
- Applebaum, S., 1964. "Jewish status at Cyrene in the Roman period." La Parola del Passato 19, 291-303. — Argues from the ephebic lists (IRCyr C.145, C.431) and the nomophylax Elazar son of Jason (C.98) that some Jews held Cyrenean citizenship. — Not online (bibliography citation only, via the IRCyr commentary).
- Applebaum, S., 1954. Hebrew article on the Jews of Cyrenaica (cited in IRCyr bibliography). — Early survey of Jewish evidence in Cyrenaica. — Not online / citation only.
- Lüderitz, G., 1983. "Corpus jüdischer Zeugnisse aus der Cyrenaika." Beihefte zum Tübinger Atlas des Vorderen Orients B 53, Wiesbaden: Reichert. — Corpus of all Jewish inscriptions of Cyrenaica, including the Cyrene ephebic lists and C.98, with discussion of which names are Jewish. — Not online (cited in IRCyr commentaries only).
- Kerkeslager, A., 2006. "The Jews in Egypt and Cyrenaica, 66-c. 235 CE." In S. T. Katz (ed.), The Cambridge History of Judaism, vol. 4, 53-68. — Covers the Jonathan revolt (73 CE) and the size and status of the Cyrenean community. — Paywalled (Cambridge); bibliographic entry only.
- Fraser, P. M., on IGCyr 063700 (honours for Ptolemy VIII, 145-116 BCE), cited by IGCyr. — Fraser suggested the dedicated building might be a synagogue with the Jews as dedicants (restoration [οἱ Ἰουδαῖοι?]). Lanciers 1988 rejected this, so it was not used as evidence. — Original publications not online; only the IGCyr summary was read.
- Reynolds, J. M., Roueché, C., Bodard, G. et al., Inscriptions of Roman Cyrenaica (IRCyr2020), https://ircyr2020.inslib.kcl.ac.uk/ — Holds the ephebic lists C.145 and C.431 and the nomophylax list C.98. — Website blocked by an anti-bot (Anubis) challenge; the same EpiDoc files were read from a GitHub mirror (olvidalo/efes-ng-ircyr) instead.
- Posen Library, "Early Jewish Diaspora Communities: Cyrene" and "Jewish Community of Cyrene", https://www.posenlibrary.com/node/11659 — Anthology of primary sources on Cyrene. — 403 Forbidden.
