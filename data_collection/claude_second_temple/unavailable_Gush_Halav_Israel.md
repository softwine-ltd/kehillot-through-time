# Unavailable sources: Gush Halav (Gischala), Israel

- Meyers, E. M., C. L. Meyers, J. F. Strange (with R. S. Hanson), 1979. "Preliminary Report on the 1977 and 1978 Seasons at Gush Halav (el-Jish)." BASOR 233: 33-58. — Stratigraphy under the synagogue; may describe Early Roman / pre-synagogue occupation and suggests abandonment after Bar Kokhba. — Not online (JSTOR paywall); known only via secondary summaries.
- Meyers, E. M., C. L. Meyers, J. F. Strange, 1990. Excavations at the Ancient Synagogue of Gush Halav. Meiron Excavation Project 5, Eisenbrauns/ASOR (incl. T. R. W. Longstaff, "Gush Halav in the Ancient Literary Sources"; J. Raynor, numismatics). — Pottery and coins of earlier periods, literary sources. — Not online (book; only TOC fetched).
- Meyers, E. M., and F. Vito, 1993/2008. "Gush Halav." New Encyclopedia of Archaeological Excavations in the Holy Land, vol. 2, 546-550. — Site history incl. Hellenistic/Early Roman, Tyrian coins, imported wares. — Not online (only quoted via deadseaquake.info).
- Wachtel, I., R. Sabar, U. Davidovich, 2017. "Tell Gush Halav during the Bronze and Iron Ages." Strata 35: 115-134. — Size estimates of the tell by period (Iron Age; possibly later periods), relevant to Year Established and site size. — Abstract only (paywall).
- Wachtel, I., et al., 2018. "Gush Halav in the Biblical Period" (Hebrew). Eretz-Israel 33. — Iron Age settlement (possible Israelite presence). — Not online.
- Davidovich, U., and I. Wachtel, 2021. "Upper Galilee, Survey: Final Report." Hadashot Arkheologiyot 133. — Survey data that may include site size by period at Gush Halav. — Not located online in this session.
- Hartal, M. (c. 2008). Gush Halav excavation report on the southern slope of the tell, Hadashot Arkheologiyot 120 (cited in HA 122). — Habitation layers of the Early and Late Roman periods. — URL not found.
- Aviam, M., 2004. Jews, Pagans and Christians in the Galilee. University of Rochester Press. — Aviam's 1985 survey discovered the line of a wall around the upper third of the hill at Gush Halav (Early Roman fortification); pottery/coins. — Paywalled book; academia.edu page 403.
- Syon, D., 2015. Small Change in Hellenistic-Roman Galilee. Israel Numismatic Society. — Site-level coin finds (possibly Hasmonean coins at Gush Halav) for dating Judaization. — Not online.
- Hamburger, H., 1954. Coin hoard from Gush Halav (cited by the Coin Hoards of the Roman Empire database, chre.ashmus.ox.ac.uk/hoard/5502). — Hoard found in a jar during house foundations; date unknown. — Not fetched / original not online.
- Rappaport, U., 2013. John of Gischala: From the Mountains of Galilee to the Walls of Jerusalem. — Scholarly analysis of Josephus on Gischala and its population/economy. — Paywalled book.
- Strange, J. F., chapter on Gush Halav in Fiensy & Strange (eds.), Galilee in the Late Second Temple and Mishnaic Periods (Fortress, 2015). — Archaeological summary of the site in the period. — Not online (only Aviam's chapter is free).
