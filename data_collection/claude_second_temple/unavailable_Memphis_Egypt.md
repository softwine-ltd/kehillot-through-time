# Unavailable sources: Memphis, Egypt (Second Temple period)

- Thompson, Dorothy J., 1988 (2nd ed. 2012). *Memphis under the Ptolemies*. Princeton University Press, ch. "Ethnic Minorities" (pp. 76-98 in 2nd ed.; p. 85 on Jews, pp. 91-93 on the Chahap stela). https://muse.jhu.edu/book/84585 — The standard treatment of the Jewish and other ethnic communities in Ptolemaic Memphis, including possible Persian-period Jewish mercenaries. — Book is paywalled; only the table of contents and reviews were seen.
- Jakobeit, Ulrike, 2016. "Das jüdische Viertel Yht in Memphis - ein weiterer Beleg." In S. L. Lippert, M. Schentuleit, M. A. Stadler (eds.), *Sapientia Felicitas: Festschrift für Günter Vittmann* (CENiM 14), Montpellier, pp. 261-269. — Gives a further Egyptian attestation of the Jewish quarter (Yht) of Memphis and probably its date. — The Geneva-hosted PDF returned "Access Denied" (403) and no other open copy was found.
- Rémondon, Roger, 1960. "Les anti-Sémites de Memphis" (on CPJ I 141). *Chronique d'Égypte* 35, pp. 244-261. — Reedition and dating of the Memphis letter on hostility to Jews (1st c. BCE). — Paywalled (Brepols); not online.
- Tcherikover, V. A., and A. Fuks, 1957. *Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum* I, nos. 132 and 141. Harvard University Press. — Text, date and commentary of the Serapeum letter to Onias (164 BCE) and the Memphis letter CPJ 141. — Not online. The papyri.info entries were blocked by a bot check.
- Hacham, N., and T. Ilan (eds.), 2020. *Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum* IV (Ptolemaic period). De Gruyter. https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110674521 — May include new Memphis/Saqqara texts naming Jews. — Paywalled.
- Galazzi, C., and P. J. Sijpesteijn, 1996. "P. Cairo Inv. JE 51509: Lists of Payment." *ZPE* 113, pp. 171-182. — Edition of SB XXIV 16272 from Saqqara, which names the Jew Annaios. — Not accessed (JSTOR/paywall).
- Stern, L., 1884. "Die bilingue Stele des Chahap." *ZÄS* 22, pp. 101-109; Schaefer, H., 1902. "Ein Phönizier auf einem aegyptischen Grabstein der Ptolemaerzeit." *ZÄS* 40, pp. 31-35. — Edition of the stela that mentions the "district of the Jews" in Memphis. — Not online (De Gruyter paywall).
- Vittmann, Günter, 2003. *Ägypten und die Fremden im ersten vorchristlichen Jahrtausend*. Mainz: Zabern, p. 70. — Discusses the Chahap stela and the Jewish quarter or Jewish Camp. — Not online.
- Szántó, Zsuzsanna, 2024. *The Jews of Ptolemaic Egypt*. De Gruyter. — Updated version of the 2016 dissertation used here; may add Memphis evidence. — Paywalled.
- Porten, Bezalel. *Mi-Migdol ad Seveneh* ("From Migdol to Syene": Documents Pertaining to Jews in Egypt in the Persian Period). Jerusalem: Bialik Institute (2020). — May list Persian-period Memphis documents naming Jews. — Not online.
- Segal, J. B., 1983. *Aramaic Texts from North Saqqâra*. London: Egypt Exploration Society. — Persian-period Aramaic texts from the Memphite necropolis, which may contain Jewish names. — Not online.
- Modrzejewski, Joseph Mélèze, 1995. *The Jews of Egypt: From Rameses II to Emperor Hadrian*. JPS / Princeton. — General survey that probably discusses Jews at Memphis. — Not online (Google Books blocked).
- Kasher, Aryeh, 1985. *The Jews in Hellenistic and Roman Egypt*. Tübingen: Mohr. — List of Jewish settlements, possibly including Memphis. — Not online.
- Winnicki, J. K., 2009. *Late Egypt and Her Neighbours*. Warsaw, pp. 180-181. — Cited by BYU Studies on the Judeans at Migdol, Tahpanhes and Memphis in Jeremiah's time. — Not online.
