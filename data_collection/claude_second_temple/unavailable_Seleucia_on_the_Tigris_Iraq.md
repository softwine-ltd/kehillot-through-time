- Goodblatt, David, 1987. "Josephus on Parthian Babylonia (Antiquities XVIII, 310-379)." Journal of the American Oriental Society, 107.4, 605-622. DOI 10.2307/603303. — Main modern study of the Seleucia massacre: identifies the rival factions (old Greco-Macedonian families vs. hellenized Babylonian citizens) and argues the brothers' episode and the Seleucia massacre were simultaneous, which affects the dating of the Jewish presence and massacre. — JSTOR paywall; relbib.de abstract page blocked by browser verification; keibi record has no abstract.
- Neusner, Jacob, 1969. A History of the Jews in Babylonia I: The Parthian Period. Leiden: Brill (2nd ed.), esp. pp. 54-57 (as cited by Encyclopaedia Iranica). — Standard account and dating of the Anilaeus/Asinaeus episode and the flight to and massacre at Seleucia; may discuss the size of the Jewish community. — Not online (Brill/Google Books).
- Encyclopaedia Iranica, "Asinaeus and Anilaeus" (Iranica Online; author not seen). https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/asinaeus-and-anilaeus-figure-in-josephus-jewish-antiquities-18 — Discusses the flight of rural Jews to Seleucia and Ctesiphon and hostility between rural and urban groups; dating. — 403 Forbidden.
- Pauly-Wissowa, Realencyclopädie, 2nd series, vol. 3 (1921), s.v. "Seleukeia (am Tigris)", pp. 1177f. — Cited by Encyclopaedia Judaica for Seleucia's Jews and population. — Not online in accessible form.
- Oppenheimer, Aharon (with B. Isaac and M. Lecker), 1983. Babylonia Judaica in the Talmudic Period. Wiesbaden: Reichert (TAVO Beiheft B 47), s.v. Seleucia / Maḥoza. — Gazetteer of Jewish settlement evidence in Babylonia including Seleucia. — Not online.
- Eshel, B. Z., 1979. Jewish Settlements in Babylonia during Talmudic Times (Hebrew). Jerusalem: Magnes. — Gazetteer entry for Seleucia/Maḥoza. — Not online.
