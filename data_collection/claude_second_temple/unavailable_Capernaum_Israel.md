# Unavailable sources: Capernaum, Israel

- Reed, Jonathan L., 1992. "The Population of Capernaum." Occasional Papers, Institute for Antiquity and Christianity (Claremont); 19 pp. https://researchworks.laverne.edu/esploro/outputs/book/The-population-of-Capernaum/991004109321106311 — Dedicated demographic study; method (area x density) and the 600-1,500 range. — Not online (catalogue entry only).
- Reed, Jonathan L., 2000. "Jesus and Capernaum: Archaeological and Gospel Stratigraphy" and "Population Numbers, Urbanization, and Economics," in Archaeology and the Galilean Jesus: A Re-examination of the Evidence (Harrisburg: Trinity Press International), pp. 139-69 (Capernaum chapter). — Source of the 600-1,500 estimate (pp. 151-152) and stone-vessel counts; Wikipedia attributes "about 1,500" to Reed 2002. — Not online (Google Books blocked); known only via secondary citations.
- Mattila, Sharon L., 2015. "Capernaum, Village of Naḥum, from Hellenistic to Byzantine Times," in D. A. Fiensy and J. R. Strange (eds.), Galilee in the Late Second Temple and Mishnaic Periods, vol. 2 (Minneapolis: Fortress), 217-57. — Most up-to-date synthesis of Hellenistic-Early Roman phases and Jewish character. — Not online (paywall).
- Chancey, Mark A., 2002. The Myth of a Gentile Galilee (SNTSMS 118; Cambridge University Press), 101-5. — Discussion of Capernaum as a predominantly Jewish village. — Paywall / Google Books.
- Aviam, Mordechai, 2013. "People, Land, Economy, and Belief in First-Century Galilee and Its Origins: A Comprehensive Archaeological Synthesis," in D. A. Fiensy and R. K. Hawkins (eds.), The Galilean Economy in the Time of Jesus (Atlanta: SBL), 11-20. — Hasmonean-period foundation of Capernaum as a Jewish village. — Not accessed (paywall; cited by Grey 2014).
- Loffreda, Stanislao and Vassilios Tzaferis, 1993. "Capernaum," in E. Stern (ed.), The New Encyclopedia of Archaeological Excavations in the Holy Land, vol. 1. — Persian-period resettlement, Hellenistic-Roman strata, 10-12 acre area, max. 1,500 inhabitants. — Not online (read only via quotations on deadseaquake.info and Grey 2014).
- Tzaferis, Vassilios, 1983. "New Archaeological Evidence on Ancient Capernaum." Biblical Archaeologist 46.4: 198-204. — Excavation results of the Greek Orthodox sector, early phases. — Paywall (JSTOR).
- Tzaferis, Vassilios (ed.), 1989. Excavations at Capernaum, vol. 1: 1978-1982 (Winona Lake: Eisenbrauns). — Final report incl. coins and pottery. — Not online.
- Corbo, V. C.; Loffreda, S.; Spijkerman, A., 1970s. Cafarnao I-IV (Jerusalem: Franciscan Printing Press). — Franciscan excavation reports: Hellenistic-Early Roman houses, pottery and coins (incl. Hasmonean coins), basalt floor under the synagogue. — Not online.
- Laughlin, John C. H., 1993. "Capernaum: From Jesus' Time and After." Biblical Archaeology Review 19:5. https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/?p=70798 — First-century village size and population (reported elsewhere as "no more than 1,000"). — Paywall (only opening paragraph visible).
- Strange, James F. and Hershel Shanks, 1983. "Synagogue Where Jesus Preached Found at Capernaum." Biblical Archaeology Review, Nov/Dec 1983. https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/article/synagogue-where-jesus-preached-found-at-capernaum/ — First-century building beneath the limestone synagogue. — Paywall (excerpt only).
- Bible Odyssey, "Capernaum." https://www.bibleodyssey.org/articles/capernaum/ — Scholarly overview of first-century village. — 403 Forbidden.
- Taylor & Francis chapters "Capernaum" (https://www.taylorfrancis.com/chapters/edit/10.4324/9780203087657-24/capernaum) and https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/9780203446348/chapters/10.4324/9780203446348-9 — Possibly population discussion. — Paywall.
