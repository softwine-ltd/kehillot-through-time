- Hengel, M. and A. M. Schwemer, 1997. "Paul between Damascus and Antioch: The Unknown Years." London: SCM / Louisville: Westminster John Knox. — Detailed treatment of the Jewish community and God-fearers of Damascus in the 30s CE, the synagogues, and the ethnarch of Aretas; may discuss community size. — Not online (only a review by C. Blomberg was read).
- Riesner, R., 1998. "Paul's Early Period: Chronology, Mission Strategy, Theology." Grand Rapids: Eerdmans. — Cited in the Encyclopedia of the Bible and Its Reception; discusses Jewish Damascus, the Nabataean ethnarch, and the dating of Paul's stay. — Not online.
- Kasher, A., 1990. "Jews and Hellenistic Cities in Eretz-Israel." TSAJ 21, Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck. — Relations of Jews with the Hellenistic cities of the region, likely including the Damascus massacre of 66 CE and its causes. — Not online (only catalogue and review records found).
- Noy, D. and H. Bloedhorn, 2004. "Inscriptiones Judaicae Orientis, vol. III: Syria and Cyprus." TSAJ 102, Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck. https://cdn.mohrsiebeck.com/46,f6be67d91b4b1bb29e67a6c0c6d2d2.pdf — Corpus of Jewish inscriptions from Graeco-Roman Syria; would show whether any Damascus Jewish inscriptions date before 74 CE. — Only a search snippet seen; not fetched/full text not accessible.
- Schürer, E. (rev. G. Vermes, F. Millar, M. Goodman), 1986. "The History of the Jewish People in the Age of Jesus Christ," vol. III.1. Edinburgh: T&T Clark. — Survey of the Diaspora in Syria including Damascus (Josephus figures, ethnarch). — Not online.
- Campbell, D. A., 2002. "An Anchor for Pauline Chronology: Paul's Flight from 'the Ethnarch of King Aretas' (2 Corinthians 11:32-33)." Journal of Biblical Literature 121: 279-302. — Dating of Paul's flight and the status of Damascus/the ethnarch c. 36-37 CE. — Paywalled (only a Duke Scholars record seen).
- Burns, R., 2005. "Damascus: A History." London/New York: Routledge. — General history of Damascus, including the Hellenistic-Roman city and its Jews. — Not online.
- Josephus, Jewish War 2.561 and 7.368, Thackeray (Loeb) translation. — Modern critical translation of the 10,500 and 18,000 figures; Greek checked on Perseus instead. — Paywalled (Loeb Classical Library).
