# Unavailable sources: Herakleopolis, Egypt

- Cowey, J. M. S. and K. Maresch, 2001. "Urkunden des Politeuma der Juden von Herakleopolis (144/3-133/2 v. Chr.) (P.Polit.Iud.)." Papyrologica Coloniensia XXIX, Wiesbaden: Westdeutscher Verlag. — The editio princeps. Its introduction (pp. 1-23) covers the earliest Jewish evidence in the nome (P.Hib. I 96, the Sedment stelae), the Jewish settlement in the harbour area and at the fortress (p. 12), and the list of names (pp. 30-32). It is the most likely place for any estimate of community size. — Not online (print monograph); known only through Kreuzer, Honigman and Sänger.
- Hacham, N. and T. Ilan (eds.), 2020. "Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum, vol. IV: The Ptolemaic Period." Berlin: De Gruyter, nos. 557-577 (pp. 86-134). https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110674521 — Revised edition and commentary of the Herakleopolis archive (Kugler, Fikhman, Szántó), possibly with other Herakleopolite Jewish texts. — Paywalled.
- Kugler, R. A., 2022. "Resolving Disputes in Second Century BCE Herakleopolis: A Study in Jewish Legal Reasoning in Hellenistic Egypt." Leiden: Brill. — A monograph on the archive that may discuss the composition and size of the community. — Paywalled; only the BMCR review (Sänger 2024) was read.
- Czajkowski, K. and S. Wackenier, 2020. "Legal Strategies of Judaeans in Herakleopolis, Middle Egypt, according to the Archives of the Politeuma." HeBAI 9: 415-434. DOI 10.1628/hebai-2020-0026 — Treats the politeuma as a social network and discusses its court's reach to Judaeans outside its membership. — Paywalled (Mohr Siebeck); the HAL copy (hal-03915640) was blocked by bot protection.
- Cowey, J. M. S. et al., 2003. "Das Archiv des Phrurarchen Dioskurides (154-145 v. Chr.?) (P.Phrur.Diosk.)." Papyrologica Coloniensia XXX, Paderborn: Schöningh. — Archive of the Herakleopolis harbour fortress just before the politeuma archive; may name Jewish soldiers or residents. — Not online.
- Qandeil, H., 2024. Article on the phrourarchoi, based on the archive of Dioskourides. Annals of the Faculty of Arts, Ain Shams University. https://aafu.journals.ekb.eg/article_367636.html — According to a search snippet, Jews served in the Herakleopolis garrison units. — HTTP 503.
- Kruse, T., 2008. "Das politeuma der Juden von Herakleopolis in Ägypten." In M. Karrer et al. (eds.), Die Septuaginta – Texte, Kontexte, Lebenswelten, WUNT 219, Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, 166-175. — Analysis of the politeuma (cited by Kreuzer). — Not online / paywalled.
- Sänger, P., 2019. "Die ptolemäische Organisationsform politeuma." TSAJ 178, Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck. — Monograph on politeumata; according to reviews, it says the Jewish politeuma lost its juridical role after the Roman conquest (relevant to the post-30 BCE fate of the community). — Paywalled; only the sehepunkte review was read.
- Kasher, A., 2002. Review of Cowey & Maresch. JQR 93: 257-268. — Discussion of the community. — Paywalled (JSTOR/Project MUSE).
- P.Hib. I 96 = CPJ I 18 (c. 260/259 BCE), papyri.info record https://papyri.info/hgv/8060 — Needed to check whether this earliest Jewish document of the area belongs to the city of Herakleopolis or to a village of the nome. — papyri.info blocked by bot protection.
- Trismegistos archive 197 (https://trismegistos.org/archive/197) and Copenhagen Associations Inventory no. 1370 (https://ancientassociations.ku.dk/assoc/1370) — Database summaries of the politeuma archive. — 403 / 503.
