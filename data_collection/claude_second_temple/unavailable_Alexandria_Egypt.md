# Unavailable sources: Alexandria, Egypt (Second Temple period)

- Delia, Diana, 1988. "The Population of Roman Alexandria." Transactions of the American Philological Association 118, pp. 275-292. — Main demographic study of Roman Alexandria; reportedly gives a Jewish share of c. 36% and total-population estimates. — JSTOR paywall; only secondhand citations seen.
- Harker, Andrew, 2008. "The status of the Alexandrian Jews" (Appendix II). In Loyalty and Dissidence in Roman Egypt: The Case of the Acta Alexandrinorum, Cambridge UP, pp. 212-220. — Discusses modern estimates (a fifth to a third of the population). — Cambridge paywall; chapter summary only.
- Mélèze Modrzejewski, Joseph, 1995. The Jews of Egypt: From Rameses II to Emperor Hadrian. Princeton UP / JPS. — Estimate of >300,000 Jews in Roman Egypt, over half in Alexandria; assessment of 38 and 66 CE events. — Not online; accessed only via COJS excerpts.
- Kasher, Aryeh, 1985. The Jews in Hellenistic and Roman Egypt: The Struggle for Equal Rights. Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck. — Alexandrian politeuma, quarters, community size. — Not online (catalogue records only).
- Barclay, John M. G., 1996. Jews in the Mediterranean Diaspora from Alexander to Trajan. Edinburgh: T&T Clark / UC Press. — Settlement in Alexandria, Pharos festival (p. 424), pogrom of 38 CE, population discussion. — Not online (publisher pages/reviews only).
- Tcherikover, V. A. & Fuks, A. (eds.), 1957-1964. Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum I-III. Harvard UP; and Hacham, N. & Ilan, T. (eds.), 2022. CPJ V. De Gruyter. — Alexandrian Jewish papyri (BGU IV 1151 etc., early Roman). — Not freely online / paywalled.
- Horbury, William & Noy, David, 1992. Jewish Inscriptions of Graeco-Roman Egypt. Cambridge UP. — Alexandrian Jewish inscriptions incl. early Ptolemaic epitaphs from the El-Ibrahimiya necropolis (JIGRE 1-12?), proseuche dedications 9 and 13. — Not online; only individual entries via AGRW/Posen.
- Alberro, Charles A., n.d. (year not verified). "The Alexandrian Jews during the Ptolemaic period." PhD dissertation, Michigan State University. https://d.lib.msu.edu/etd/34366 — Ptolemaic Alexandrian Jewry, Delta quarter, population estimates. — 403 / repository access restricted.
- Ismail, Shaza, n.d. (2015 per a search snippet, not verified). Article in Journal of the Faculty of Tourism and Hotels, Helwan University (jaauth.journals.ekb.eg article_67232). — Cites Delia's 36% Jewish share, total population >350,000. — 503 / connection reset.
- Sly, Dorothy I., 1996. Philo's Alexandria. London: Routledge (and review at pure.royalholloway.ac.uk/files/890846/Sly.pdf). — Population density estimates for Alexandria and its Jews. — Book not online; review PDF 403.
- Gambetti, Sandra, 2009. The Alexandrian Riots of 38 C.E. and the Persecution of the Jews. Leiden: Brill (JSJSup 135). — Delta district, Jewish residence, 38-41 CE. — Brill paywall; BMCR review only.
- Fraser, P. M., 1972. Ptolemaic Alexandria. Oxford: Clarendon. — Jewish archeion, early Jewish necropolis, Jewish population. — Not online.
- Donaldson, Terence L. (ed.), 2000. Religious Rivalries and the Struggle for Success in Caesarea Maritima, ch. 12; Redies, Michael, 2006. "Acta Alexandrinorum." Brill's New Pauly. — Cited by Wikipedia for the c. 35% Jewish share. — Not checked (paywall / not online).
- Brand, Ezra. "Basilica, Stoa, and Cathedra" (on Sukkah 51b, the Great Synagogue of Alexandria). — Ptolemaic-era great synagogue. — Not accessed (cited by Wikipedia only).
