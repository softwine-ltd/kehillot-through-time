# Unavailable sources: Beth Zur, Palestine (Second Temple period)

- Sellers, O. R., P. W. Lapp, N. L. Lapp, R. W. Funk, J. L. McKenzie, 1968. "The 1957 Excavation at Beth-Zur." AASOR 38. Cambridge, MA: ASOR. archive.org item 1957excavationat0000ovid. — Primary excavation report: Funk's history of the defenses (pp. 4-17), P. & N. Lapp on Iron II-Hellenistic pottery groups (p. 70), P. Lapp on Field II (p. 29); stratigraphy and extent of the Persian, Hellenistic and Hasmonean occupation. — Lending-library only (archive.org access-restricted, text download 401); not online otherwise.
- Sellers, O. R., 1933. "The Citadel of Beth-Zur." Philadelphia: Westminster. HathiTrust uc1.b4495314 (marked full view); archive.org citadelofbethzur0000sell. — 1931 excavation report: Hellenistic citadel phases, coins (Hezekiah coin, p. 73 f., pl. XIV:9; Hasmonean coins), date of abandonment. — HathiTrust 403 twice; archive.org copy access-restricted.
- Sellers, O. R. and W. F. Albright, 1931. "The First Campaign of Excavation at Beth-zur." BASOR 43: 2-13. — Preliminary results, Persian/Hellenistic coins and occupation. — JSTOR/Chicago Journals paywall.
- Funk, R. W., 1993. "Beth-Zur." In E. Stern (ed.), NEAEHL 1: 259-261. — Standard summary of occupation phases (very sparse Persian/early Hellenistic occupation; Hasmonean fortress; abandonment). — Not online.
- Funk, R. W., 1958. "The 1957 Campaign at Beth-zur." BASOR 150: 8-20. — Interim results of the 1957 season. — JSTOR paywall.
- Sellers, O. R., 1958. "The 1957 Campaign at Beth-zur." Biblical Archaeologist 21. — Interim results (cited in search snippets). — JSTOR paywall.
- Reich, R., 1992. "The Beth-Zur Citadel II - A Persian Residency?" Tel Aviv 19: 113-123. — Argues the citadel was a Persian-period residency (significant Persian-period activity). — Taylor & Francis paywall; not found online.
- Carter, C. E., 1999. "The Emergence of Yehud in the Persian Period: A Social and Demographic Study." JSOTSup 294, p. 157. — Site-by-site size/population data for Persian Yehud, may give an estimate for Beth-zur (judges it very sparsely inhabited). — Book, not online (cited via Finkelstein 2018).
- Stern, E., 2001. "Archaeology of the Land of the Bible II: The Assyrian, Babylonian, and Persian Periods," pp. 437-438; and Stern 1982, "Material Culture of the Land of the Bible in the Persian Period," p. 36. — Argues for significant Persian-period activity at Beth-zur. — Books, not online.
- Finkelstein, I., 2010. "The Territorial Extent and Demography of Yehud/Judea in the Persian and Early Hellenistic Periods." Revue Biblique 117: 39-54. — Demographic estimates for Yehud/Judea; Beth-zur as southern border. — Paywalled (content reprinted in Finkelstein 2018, which was read).
- Sukenik, E. L., 1934. "Paralipomena Palaestinensia: The Oldest Coins of Judaea." JPOS 14: 178-184. — Identification of the Beth-zur Hezekiah coin as Yehud coinage. — Not online.
- Ronen, Y., 1998. "The Weight Standards of the Judean Coinage in the Late Persian and Early Ptolemaic Period." Near Eastern Archaeology 61(2): 122-126. — Dating of the Hezekiah coin found at Beth-zur. — JSTOR paywall (cited by Wikipedia).
- Negev, A. and S. Gibson (eds.), 2001. "Archaeological Encyclopedia of the Holy Land," pp. 89-90, s.v. Beth Zur. — Summary of phases (Hellenistic peak, 3rd c. BCE citadel, abandonment by 100 BCE). — Book, not online (cited by Wikipedia).
