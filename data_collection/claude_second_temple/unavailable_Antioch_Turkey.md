- Kraeling, C. H., 1932. "The Jewish Community at Antioch." Journal of Biblical Literature 51(2): 130-160. — The foundational population estimate (variously reported as 45,000 in Augustus' time, out of c. 300,000; and as a peak of 60,000-65,000) and the location of Jewish quarters (Kerateion, Daphne, plain). — JSTOR paywall; only cited secondhand (Eng 2018; Antiochepedia blog; search snippets).
- Meeks, W. A. & Wilken, R. L., 1978. Jews and Christians in Antioch in the First Four Centuries of the Common Era. SBL Sources for Biblical Study 13. Missoula: Scholars Press, p. 8. — Cited by Eng (2018) for 45,000 Jews under Augustus; method and range unchecked. — Not online (catalog records only).
- Dunn, J. D. G., 1983. "The Incident at Antioch (Gal. 2:11-18)." Journal for the Study of the New Testament 5(18): 3-57. https://doi.org/10.1177/0142064X8300501801 — Reportedly cites "at least 45,000" Jews out of c. 300,000 under Augustus (per search snippet). — 403 / paywall.
- Anonymous/unknown author, "Christians and Jews in Antioch." academia.edu 11295312. — Reportedly gives Kraeling's estimate of c. 65,000 Jews in a city of c. 500,000 (5-10%). — 403 (academia.edu).
- Zetterholm, M., 2003. The Formation of Christianity in Antioch. London: Routledge, ch. "The Jewish Community in Antioch up to 66 C.E." — Likely discusses population size, legal status, synagogues. — Not online (publisher pages only).
- Brooten, B. J., 2000. "The Jews of Ancient Antioch." In C. Kondoleon (ed.), Antioch: The Lost Ancient City, Princeton, 29-37. — Social profile, synagogues (cited by Eng). — Not online.
- Downey, G., 1961. A History of Antioch in Syria from Seleucus to the Arab Conquest. Princeton UP. — Jewish community history, Kerateion, Malalas riot of 39/40 CE. — Not online (only Nock's GRBS review read).
- Kasher, A., 1982. "The Rights of the Jews of Antioch on the Orontes." Proceedings of the American Academy for Jewish Research 49: 69-85. — Citizenship question. — Paywall (JSTOR).
- Finkelstein, A. B., 2024. "Antiochene Riots against Jews in Malalas's Chronicle." In A. U. De Giorgi (ed.), Antioch on the Orontes, Cambridge UP, ch. 19. — The 39/40 CE riot (third year of Gaius) and other riots. — Paywall; abstract only.
- Hartin, P. J., 1991. "Jewish Christianity: Focus on Antioch in the First Century." Scriptura 36. https://scriptura.journals.ac.za/pub/article/view/1819 — May contain population figures. — PDF is a scanned image with no text layer (no OCR available).
- Barclay, J. M. G., 1996. Jews in the Mediterranean Diaspora from Alexander to Trajan. Edinburgh: T&T Clark, Syria chapter. — Antioch community, riots of 40 and 67 CE. — Not online.
- Krauss, S., 1902. "Antioche." Revue des Études Juives 45: 27-49. — Jewish quarters and history. — Not accessed (not found online).
- Bibleodyssey.org, "The Jews of Antioch after the Fall of Jerusalem." — Post-70 status, Titus petition. — 403.
