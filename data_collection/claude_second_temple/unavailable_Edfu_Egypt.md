# Unavailable sources: Edfu (Apollinopolis Magna), Egypt

- Tcherikover, V. A., Fuks, A. (eds.), 1960. Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum, vol. II (Edfu section, CPJ II 160-408, introduction by D. M. Lewis). Harvard University Press. — The main edition of the Edfu Jewish ostraca (249 ostraca); its introduction covers the "Jewish quarter", dating (Schwartz's redating) and the families attested. — Not online (print only); papyri.info texts blocked by a bot check.
- Hacham, N., Ilan, T. (eds.), 2022. Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum, vol. V: The Early-Roman Period (30 BCE-117 CE). De Gruyter. DOI 10.1515/9783110787764. — New Demotic (CPJ V 620-626) and Greek (627-632) Edfu ostraca; introduction re-examines the date and setting of the Jewish settlement at Edfu. — Paywalled (De Gruyter 405); known only from the BMCR and Judaica reviews.
- Hacham, N., Ilan, T. (eds.), 2020. Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum, vol. IV: The Ptolemaic Period. De Gruyter. DOI 10.1515/9783110674521. — May contain Ptolemaic Jewish documents from Edfu, which could give an earlier dated presence. — Paywalled.
- Williams, M. H., 2020 (c.). "The Jews of Apollinopolis Magna/Edfu: a late-first-century CE Jewish community in Upper Egypt re-examined." In Israel in Egypt: The Land of Egypt as Concept and Reality for Jews in Antiquity and the Early Medieval Period (Brill), ch. 12, pp. 326ff. — The fullest modern study of the community; may give its size and chronology. — Paywalled; only the table of contents was seen in search results.
- LaCoste, N., 2018. Waters of the Exodus: Jewish Experiences with Water in Ptolemaic and Roman Egypt. Brill (JSJSup 190), ch. 1 section 1.1 "Edfu (Apollonopolis Magna)". — Regional survey of the Edfu evidence. — Paywalled; only the table of contents was seen.
- Kasher, A., 1985. The Jews in Hellenistic and Roman Egypt: The Struggle for Equal Rights. Mohr Siebeck, pp. 161-167. — Cited by Struffolino for Jews at Apollinopolis Magna as village scribes and in administrative and military roles (probably Ptolemaic evidence). — Not online.
- Szántó, Zs., 2024. The Jews of Ptolemaic Egypt: The History of a Diaspora Community in Light of the Papyri. De Gruyter. — May discuss Ptolemaic Jewish evidence from Edfu and Jewish names in Demotic. — Paywalled.
- Manteuffel, J., Michałowski, K. et al., 1937-1950. Tell Edfou I-III (Fouilles franco-polonaises, Rapports). IFAO, Cairo. — Editio princeps of the Edfu ostraca, including those from the Jewish district (cf. Tell Edfou I, 145ff., II, 146ff.). — Not online (IFAO catalogue only).
- Bernand, A., 1972. Le Paneion d'El-Kanaïs: les inscriptions grecques. Brill. — Edition of the two Jewish graffiti near Edfu (I.Kanaïs 34, 42). — Not online.
- Kerkeslager, A., 2006. "Jews in Egypt and Cyrenaica, 66-c. 235 CE." Cambridge History of Judaism, vol. 4. — Discusses the Edfu Jewish tax ostraca and the community after 70 CE. — Paywalled (Cambridge).
