# Unavailable sources: Ostia, Italy (Second Temple period)

- Runesson, A., 2001. "The synagogue at ancient Ostia: the building and its history from the first to the fifth century." In B. Olsson, D. Mitternacht, O. Brandt (eds), The Synagogue of Ancient Ostia and the Jews of Rome (ActaRom-4°, 57), Stockholm. https://ecsi.se/actarom-4-57/ — Argues the synagogue was founded in the second half of the 1st c. CE; detailed phasing and reading of the Mindius Faustus inscription. — Only abstract/contents online.
- Runesson, A. (year not verified). "The Oldest Original Synagogue Building in the Diaspora: A Response to L. Michael White." Harvard Theological Review. https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/harvard-theological-review/article/oldest-original-synagogue-building-in-the-diaspora-a-response-to-l-michael-white/8F546FE902FC8A68E9CB58E09BCECD79 — Case for a 1st-c. CE synagogue phase; dates Plotius Fortunatus inscription 1st-2nd c. — Only extract and footnotes visible; full text paywalled.
- White, L. M., 1997. "Synagogue and Society in Imperial Ostia: Archaeological and Epigraphic Evidence." Harvard Theological Review 90.1, 23-58. — Redates the building to Trajan/Hadrian and argues it began as a private insula; discusses Jewish inscriptions of Ostia/Castel Porziano. — Paywalled (abstract only).
- Nongbri, B., 2015. "Archival Research on the Excavation of the Synagogue at Ostia: A Preliminary Report." Journal for the Study of Judaism 46, 366-402. DOI 10.1163/15700631-12340105 — Coin evidence for the building's phases. — Paywalled.
- D'Alessio, A. and Zevi, F. (eds), 2023. La Sinagoga di Ostia Antica: 60 anni dalla scoperta. — Latest excavation results (UT-OSMAP) dating the complex no earlier than the early 3rd c. CE; possibly the Pianabella Iudaei inscription. — Not online.
- Publication of the Pianabella "Iudaei" inscription (found 2006; Q. Fabius Longor[um]) — Primary edition with date (claimed early 1st c. CE). — Could not identify or access the epigraphic edition; known only via press release and blog.
- Noy, D., 1993. Jewish Inscriptions of Western Europe, vol. I (JIWE I 13-18). Cambridge UP. — Editions and dates of Ostia Jewish inscriptions (Mindius Faustus, C. Iulius Iustus, Plotius Fortunatus). — Not online; heurist Judaism and Rome entry returned 503.
- Frey, J.-B., Corpus Inscriptionum Iudaicarum I, no. 533 (Castel Porziano inscription of C. Iulius Iustus, gerusiarch). — Date of the earliest community-level inscription. — Not online.
- Leon, H. J., 1960. The Jews of Ancient Rome. Philadelphia. — Background on Jews of Rome/Ostia. — Not online.
- Pavolini, C. (cited in Runesson HTR) — Holds that "a first building phase goes back to the middle of the first century". — Not accessed.
