- Lüderitz, G., 1983. *Corpus jüdischer Zeugnisse aus der Cyrenaika* (with an appendix by J. M. Reynolds). Beihefte zum Tübinger Atlas des Vorderen Orients B 53. Wiesbaden: Reichert. Nos. 70-72 (Berenike). — The standard edition and commentary of the three Berenice Jewish inscriptions, including their dates. — Not online (print only).
- Applebaum, S., 1979. *Jews and Greeks in Ancient Cyrene*. Leiden: Brill (esp. p. 160). — Wikipedia cites it for a Jewish community at Berenice near the city's founding and for its social make-up (wealthy stratum, many poor members); main monograph on Cyrenaican Jewry from Ptolemy I to 117 CE. — Not online / paywalled.
- Applebaum, S., 1961. Hebrew article on a Jewish inscription from Berenice in Cyrenaica (cited in the IRCyr bibliography as applebaum1961). — Commentary on the Berenice politeuma inscriptions. — Not online; the IRCyr pages were blocked by an anti-bot wall (Anubis).
- Reynolds, J. M., et al., *Inscriptions of Roman Cyrenaica* (IRCyr 2020), nos. B.45, B.75, B.76. https://ircyr2020.inslib.kcl.ac.uk/en/inscriptions/B.76.html — Editions and commentary of the Berenice Jewish decrees (Actian-era dating; number of archons 7, 9 and 10). — Blocked (Anubis anti-bot page), seen only as search snippets.
- Lüderitz, G., 1994. "What is the Politeuma?" In J. W. van Henten and P. W. van der Horst (eds.), *Studies in Early Jewish Epigraphy*, AGJU 21, Leiden: Brill, pp. 183-225. — Key discussion of the Berenice politeuma and its institutions. — Paywalled / not online.
- Zuckerman, C., 1985/88. "Hellenistic politeumata and the Jews: A Reconsideration." *Scripta Classica Israelica* 8-9. https://scriptaclassica.org/index.php/sci/article/view/4675 — Discusses Jewish politeumata including Berenice. — PDF is a scanned image without a text layer, not read.
- Kenrick, P. M., Bailey, D. M., et al., *Excavations at Sidi Khrebish, Benghazi (Berenice)*, Libya Antiqua Suppl. V, vols. I-IV (1977-1985). https://library.oapen.org/handle/20.500.12657/90744 — Excavation reports for Hellenistic-Roman Berenice; might contain Jewish material evidence. — Not checked: large PDFs, and searches turned up nothing Jewish.
- Kasher, A., 1985. *The Jews in Hellenistic and Roman Egypt* (with discussion of Cyrenaican politeumata). Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck. — Argues for Jewish politeuma status at Berenice. — Not online.
