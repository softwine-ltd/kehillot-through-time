# Unavailable sources: Leontopolis, Egypt

- Capponi, L., 2007. *Il tempio di Leontopoli in Egitto: identità politica e religiosa dei Giudei di Onia (c. 150 a.C.-73 d.C.).* Pisa: ETS. — Monograph on the Oniad community; ~91 epitaphs, the claim of a significant pre-temple Jewish population around Heliopolis, Jewish villages of the Land of Onias. — Not online; read only via the BMCR 2008.09.37 review.
- Capponi, L., 2025. "Leontopolis, temple of." Oxford Classical Dictionary (online), DOI 10.1093/acrefore/9780199381135.013.9157. — Up-to-date summary; rejects the Tell el-Yahudiyeh identification and puts the temple at Heliopolis; describes a Jewish military community. — Abstract only (iris.unipv.it); full entry paywalled.
- Piotrkowski, M.M., 2019. *Priests in Exile: The History of the Temple of Onias and its Community in the Hellenistic Period.* Berlin: De Gruyter. — Full study of the Oniad settlements, the Tell el-Yahudiyeh politeuma, its epitaphs and depopulation c. 115-117 CE. — Paywalled; read only via the Ancient Jew Review book note and Wikipedia.
- Horbury, W. and Noy, D., 1992. *Jewish Inscriptions of Graeco-Roman Egypt* (JIGRE), nos. 29-115. Cambridge UP. — Edition and dating of the Leontopolis/Tell el-Yahudiyeh epitaphs, with regnal-year dates and ages at death. — Not online (Cambridge, paywalled).
- Noy, D., 1994. "The Jewish Communities of Leontopolis and Venosa." In J.W. van Henten and P.W. van der Horst (eds.), *Studies in Early Jewish Epigraphy*, Leiden: Brill, 162-182. — Summary of the epigraphic evidence for the size and character of the Leontopolis community. — Paywalled / not online.
- Ameling, W., 2008. "Die jüdische Gemeinde von Leontopolis nach den Inschriften." In M. Karrer et al. (eds.), *Die Septuaginta: Texte, Kontexte, Lebenswelten* (WUNT 219), Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, 117-133. — Dates the epitaphs (e.g. Demas to 24 CE) and discusses how the community was organised. — Not online (cited by Askin 2021 and Sänger).
- Sänger, P., 2015. "Considerations on the administrative organisation of the Jewish military colony in Leontopolis: a case of generosity and calculation." In J. Tolan (ed.), *Religion and Law in Medieval Christian and Muslim Societies* vol. 5 (Brepols). — Politeuma organisation of the colony. — Paywalled; known only from a search snippet.
- Naville, E., 1890. *The Mound of the Jew and the City of Onias* (EEF Memoir 7). — 1887 excavation; Romano-Jewish town, suburb and cemetery, epitaph finds. — Heidelberg digital copy blocked by bot protection (Anubis).
- Petrie, W.M.F., 1906. *Hyksos and Israelite Cities.* London: BSAE. — 1905-06 excavation of the presumed Onias temple and town at Tell el-Yahudiyeh. — UCL Digital Egypt page 403; full text not fetched.
- Ashmawy Ali, A., 2016. "Preliminary Report on the SCA Excavation at Tell el-Yahudia Season 2010-2011." *Ägypten und Levante* 26: 17-32. — Graeco-Roman houses in the northern (smaller) tell, possibly the Jewish suburb. — Paywalled (austriaca.at not fetched).
- Edgar, C.C., 1920. "Tomb-stones from Tell el Yahoudieh." *ASAE* 19; Lietzmann, H., 1923. "Jüdisch-griechische Inschriften aus Tell el Yehudieh." *ZNW* 22: 280-286. — First editions and dating of the epitaphs. — Not online.
- Bohak, G., 1995. "CPJ III 520: The Egyptian Reaction to Onias' Temple." *JSJ* 26: 32-41. — Egyptian reaction to the Oniad settlement; Ptolemaic military role. — Paywalled.
- Hayward, R., 1982. "The Jewish Temple at Leontopolis: A Reconsideration." *JJS* 33: 429-443. — Temple and community. — Paywalled.
- Kasher, A., 1985. *The Jews in Hellenistic and Roman Egypt.* Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck. — Oniad military settlement, Chelkias inscription (CPJ III 1450 = JIGRE 129). — Not online.
- Mélèze Modrzejewski, J., 1995. *The Jews of Egypt from Rameses II to Emperor Hadrian*, esp. 121-133. — Oniad colony; temple closure. — Only COJS excerpts read; book not online.
- Tcherikover, V. and Fuks, A., 1957. *Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum* I, 44-46 and no. 132. — Land of Onias; possible Onias papyrus of 164 BCE. — Not online.
- van der Horst, P.W., [Polish open-access journal article on Jewish epitaphs / age at death using JIGRE], ejournals.eu. — Ages at death from the Leontopolis epitaphs. — HTTP 503.
- Smallwood, E.M. (ed.), *Josephus: The Jewish War* (Penguin), p. 460 n. 58. — Dates the closure to c. March-August 73 CE (per Wikipedia). — Not online.
