- Yeivin, Z., 2000. "The Synagogue at Korazim: The 1962-1964, 1980-1987 Excavations." IAA Reports 10, Jerusalem (with N. May, D. Ariel). https://zenon.dainst.org/Record/000050829 — Final excavation report; likely holds the stratigraphy and coin evidence for the earliest (1st/2nd c. CE) settlement on the northern hill. — Not online (print monograph).
- Yeivin, Z., 1987. "Ancient Chorazin Comes Back to Life." Biblical Archaeology Review 13:5 (Sept/Oct 1987). — Excavator's account of the town, its size and phases. — old.biblicalarchaeology.org host not reachable from this session; BAS library paywall.
- Yeivin, Z., 1993. "Chorazin." In E. Stern (ed.), New Encyclopedia of Archaeological Excavations in the Holy Land, vol. 1. — Dating of the earliest settlement (read only via the deadseaquake.info summary). — Not online.
- Kohn-Tavor, A., 2025. "Site-Seeing: Digging Through Time at Chorazin." Biblical Archaeology Review. https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/department/site-seeing-digging-through-time-at-chorazin/ — Details of the 1st-century pottery/coins and possible late Second Temple synagogue beneath the 4th-c. synagogue. — Paywall (only intro visible).
- Reed, J. L., 2000. Archaeology and the Galilean Jesus: A Re-examination of the Evidence. Trinity Press International (chapter "Population numbers in Galilee: urbanization and economics"). — May contain size/population estimates for Galilean villages including Chorazin. — Not online (book; repository page shows no text).
- Ilan, Z., 1991. Ancient Synagogues in Israel (Hebrew). — Suggestion (cited by Encyclopaedia Judaica) that Ory's western building was the Early Roman synagogue of Chorazin. — Not online.
- Ory, J., 1926. Unpublished report to the British Mandate Department of Antiquities on Kerazeh. — Description of an earlier synagogue c. 200 m west of the later one, possibly of the late Second Temple period. — Unpublished/not online.
- Runesson, A., Binder, D. D., Olsson, B., 2008. The Ancient Synagogue from its Origins to 200 C.E.: A Source Book. Brill. — Cited by Wikipedia for Chorazin; may discuss evidence for a pre-70 synagogue. — Brill, not accessible.
- Rasmussen, C., c. 2022. Blog posts (holylandphotos.org) on the Kohn-Tavor excavation under the Chorazin synagogue floor. — Primary report of the 1st-c. pottery and coins. — Not fetched (found only via ABR summary).
- Creighton University, "Chorazin" introduction. https://moses.creighton.edu/vr/Chorazin/txt/intro.html — States 1st-c. village was likely on the northern slope and describes it as a small Jewish village. — HTTP 503.
