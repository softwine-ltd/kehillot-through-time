# Unavailable sources: Nippur, Iraq (Second Temple period)

- Coogan, Michael D., 1974. "Life in the Diaspora: Jews at Nippur in the Fifth Century B.C." Biblical Archaeologist 37, 6-12. — Classic survey of Jews in the Murašû texts (occupations, names, communities around Nippur). — Not online (catalogue records only, JSTOR paywall).
- Coogan, Michael D., 1976. West Semitic Personal Names in the Murašû Documents. Harvard Semitic Monographs 7. Missoula: Scholars Press. — Full onomastic catalogue; counts of Yahwistic names. — Not online.
- Coogan, Michael D., 1973. "Patterns in Jewish Personal Names in the Babylonian Diaspora." Journal for the Study of Judaism 4, 183-191. — Yaw-names absent before the Exile; Nippur Jewish names. — Brill paywall (catalogue record only).
- Zadok, Ran, 1978. "The Nippur Region during the Late Assyrian, Chaldean and Achaemenian Periods Chiefly according to Written Sources." Israel Oriental Studies 8, 266-332. — Settlements and ethnic groups (incl. Judeans) of the Nippur region; possibly proportions of non-Babylonian names. — Not online (catalogue record only).
- Zadok, Ran, 1979. The Jews in Babylonia during the Chaldean and Achaemenian Periods according to the Babylonian Sources. Haifa: University of Haifa. — Prosopography and Judean "clans" in the Nippur region; possibly quantitative estimates of the Jewish share of Murašû clients. — Not online.
- Zadok, Ran, 2014. "Judeans in Babylonia - Updating the Dossier." In U. Gabbay and S. Secunda (eds.), Encounters by the Rivers of Babylon (TSAJ 160), Mohr Siebeck, 109-129. — Edition/discussion of N 4518 (Judean slave sale in Nippur, Darius I) and other Nippur texts. — Paywalled, not accessed.
- Zadok, Ran, 2015. "West Semitic Groups in the Nippur Region between c. 750 and 330 B.C.E." In J. Stökl and C. Waerzeggers (eds.), Exile and Return (BZAW 478), De Gruyter. — Quantitative onomastic survey of West Semitic (incl. Judean) groups around Nippur. — Not accessed (paywall).
- Stolper, Matthew W., 1976. "A Note on Yahwistic Personal Names in the Murašû Texts." BASOR 222, 25-28. — Yahwistic names in the archive. — JSTOR paywall (bibliographic record only).
- Stolper, Matthew W., 1985. Entrepreneurs and Empire: The Murašû Archive, the Murašû Firm, and Persian Rule in Babylonia. Leiden: NINO. — Standard study of the archive, its settlements and clients. — Not online.
- Bickerman, Elias J., 1978. "The Generation of Ezra and Nehemiah." Proceedings of the American Academy for Jewish Research 45, 1-28. — Judean names in the Murašû archive (intergenerational rise of Yahwistic names). — JSTOR paywall.
- Daiches, Samuel, 1910. The Jews in Babylonia in the Time of Ezra and Nehemiah according to Babylonian Inscriptions. London: Jews' College. — First study of Jews in the Murašû tablets. — Not located online (not searched further).
- Purvis, James D., "Exile and Return: From the Babylonian Destruction to the Reconstruction of the Jewish State." In H. Shanks (ed.), Ancient Israel (Biblical Archaeology Society). — Source of the claim of Jewish communities in 28 settlements in the Nippur area (cited by Kesher journal). — Original not checked.
- Schniedewind, William M., 2004. How the Bible Became a Book. Cambridge University Press. — Source of the "about eighty distinctively Jewish personal names" figure (cited by Kesher journal). — Paywall/Google Books, not checked.
- Rabinovich, Abraham, 2009. "Info gathering on the exiled." Jerusalem Post, 16 July 2009. — Search snippets attribute to it the claims that only ~3% of Bit Murashu clients had clearly Jewish names and ~120 of 600 persons bore Jewish names (the latter apparently about the Al-Yahudu tablets); article body did not load and a mirror (jewishrefugees.org.uk) returned 403, so not used. — Not accessed (body not loaded / 403).
- Alstola, Tero, 2019. Prosopographical Database of Judeans in the Murašû Archive. Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.3351259. — Per-person data (places of residence/writing) for the 61 Judeans. — Not fetched (dataset; book chapter used instead).
- Ephʿal, Israel, 1978. "The Western Minorities in Babylonia in the 6th-5th Centuries B.C.: Maintenance and Cohesion." Orientalia NS 47, 74-90. — Settlement of deportees in Nippur-region twin towns. — Paywall/not online.
- Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft (WiBiLex), entry at https://www.die-bibel.de/stichwort/28189, surfaced in searches on Coogan and the Murašû Jews. — Scholarly lexicon overview, possibly with name counts. — 403 Forbidden.
