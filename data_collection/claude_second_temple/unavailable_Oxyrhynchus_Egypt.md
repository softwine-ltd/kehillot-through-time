- Epp, Eldon Jay, 2006. "The Jews and the Jewish Community in Oxyrhynchus: Socio-Religious Context for the New Testament Papyri." In T. J. Kraus and T. Nicklas (eds.), New Testament Manuscripts: Their Texts and Their World (Leiden: Brill), 13-52. — The main survey of the documentary papyri on Jews at Oxyrhynchus; likely lists every 1st-century CE Jewish attestation in the city (and any early Jewish quarter). — Brill paywall; catalogue record and JTS review only (relbib page blocked by browser check).
- Piotrkowski, Meron M., 2018. "'Literary Jews'. The Jewish community of Oxyrhynchus in light of non-documentary texts on papyrus." In Sources and Interpretation in Ancient Judaism: Studies for Tal Ilan at Sixty (AJEC 104; Leiden: Brill), 143-173. — Discusses the Jewish LXX papyri of Oxyrhynchus (incl. 1st-c. CE P.Oxy. 3522) and what they say about the community. — Brill paywall; bibliographic record only.
- Piotrkowski, Meron M., forthcoming. Jewish Oxyrhynchus: The History of the Jewish Community of Oxyrhynchus Before and After 117 CE. — Monograph on the whole history of the community, including pre-117 evidence. — Not yet published / not online.
- Chapa, Juan, 2021. "The 'Jewish' LXX Papyri from Oxyrhynchus: Witnesses of Ways that did not Part?" Scandinavian Journal of the Old Testament 35.2, 207-229. DOI 10.1080/09018328.2021.1976519. — Assesses which Oxyrhynchus LXX papyri (P.Oxy. 3522, 5101, 4443 etc.) are Jewish and their dates. — Taylor & Francis paywall; record page without abstract.
- Tcherikover, V. A. and A. Fuks, 1960. Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum, vol. II (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press). — Early Roman (30 BCE-117 CE) Jewish papyri, including those from Oxyrhynchus and the Oxyrhynchite nome (e.g. CPJ 414 = P.Oxy. X 1281, 21 CE). — Not online.
- Hacham, Noah and Tal Ilan (eds.), 2022. Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum, vol. V: The Early-Roman Period (Berlin: De Gruyter). — Jewish papyri of 30 BCE-117 CE published since 1957, possibly including new Oxyrhynchus texts. — De Gruyter paywall; only reviews read (judaica.ch), which did not detail Oxyrhynchus items.
- Hacham, Noah and Tal Ilan (eds.), 2020. Corpus Papyrorum Judaicarum, vol. IV: The Ptolemaic Period (Berlin: De Gruyter). — Would show any Ptolemaic Jewish attestation at Oxyrhynchus. — Paywall.
- Kasher, Aryeh, 1985. The Jews in Hellenistic and Roman Egypt: The Struggle for Equal Rights (Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck), esp. 150-51, 156-57. — Cited (by Smoot and Muhlestein, BYU Studies 61:2) for a Roman-period, politically organised and legally recognised Jewish community at Oxyrhynchus; may give dates. — Not online.
- Parsons, P. J., 1983. "3522. LXX Job 42:11-12." The Oxyrhynchus Papyri L (London: EES), 1-3. — Edition and dating of the 1st-c. CE Jewish Job fragment. — Oxford portal returned 403; volume not found online.
- Colomo, D. and W. B. Henry, 2011. "5101. LXX, Psalms." The Oxyrhynchus Papyri LXXVII (London: EES), 1-11. — Edition and dating of the Psalter with paleo-Hebrew Tetragrammaton. — Not online.
- Jones, B. W. and J. E. G. Whitehorne, 1983. Register of Oxyrhynchites, 30 B.C.-A.D. 96 (American Studies in Papyrology 25). — Prosopography of 1st-century Oxyrhynchites; would list Jewish residents by name. — Not online.
- Porten, B. and A. Yardeni, 1999. Textbook of Aramaic Documents from Ancient Egypt, vol. 4 (Jerusalem), Introduction, VI. — Lists a Ptolemaic Aramaic ostracon from Oxyrhynchus (possibly Jewish). — Not online.
- van der Vliet, J. and K. A. Worp / Balamoshev, C., Journal of Juristic Papyrology 47 (2017), article on the Jews of Oxyrhynchos and the strategos (309 CE). — Later-period organisation of the community; may summarise earlier evidence. — PDF returned 403.
