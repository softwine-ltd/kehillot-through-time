# Unavailable sources: Kerch (Panticapaeum), Ukraine

- Aybabin, A. I., 2023. "Συναγωγή τῶν Ιουδαίων καὶ θεοσεβῶν (The Community of Jews and God-Fearing) in Pantikapaion-Bosporos." Materials in Archaeology, History and Ethnography of Tauria 28, 497-509. DOI 10.29039/2413-189x.2023.28.497-509. — Full discussion of the six manumissions (57 CE to 2nd c.), which one is dated 57 CE, and the menorah finds in the fishermen's quarter. — Abstract only (full text not found online).
- Aybabin, A. I., and Sidorenko, V. A., 2008. "Novaya iudeiskaya manumissiya iz Pantikapeya" [A new Jewish manumission inscription from Panticapaeum]. Bosporskie issledovaniya 17, 121-127. — Edition and exact dating of the Bosporsky Lane manumission (reign of Kotys I, "synagogue of Phonios"/God-fearers). — Not online (citation only; academia.edu 403).
- Gibson, E. Leigh, 1999. The Jewish Manumission Inscriptions of the Bosporus Kingdom. TSAJ 75. Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck. — Standard edition and dating of CIRB 70-73 and other Bosporan Jewish manumissions. — Paywalled; publisher sample PDF 404.
- Struve, V. V. (ed.), 1965. Corpus Inscriptionum Regni Bosporani (CIRB/KBN), nos. 70-73. — Primary editions and dates of the Panticapaeum manumissions. — Not online.
- Noy, D., Panayotov, A., and Bloedhorn, H., 2004. Inscriptiones Judaicae Orientis I: Eastern Europe. TSAJ 101. — Commentary and dates for IJO I BS5-BS7 etc. (Panticapaeum). — Paywalled.
- Levinskaya, I., 1996. The Book of Acts in Its Diaspora Setting (The Book of Acts in Its First Century Setting 5). Eerdmans. — Chapter on the Bosporan Kingdom, Jews and God-fearers in Panticapaeum. — Not online.
- Levinskaya, I., and Tokhtas'ev, S. R., 1996. "Jews and Jewish Names in the Bosporan Kingdom." Te'uda 12. — Possible early (pre-1st c. CE) Jewish names in Panticapaeum. — Not online.
- Yailenko, V. P., 2022. [Epigraphic sources on Bosporan Judaism, 1st-5th c. AD]. Problemy istorii, filologii, kul'tury 4 (78), 147-174. — Systematic review of Jewish inscriptions of the Bosporus incl. Panticapaeum 1st-c. manumissions. — Journal site/PDF 404.
- [Authors not seen], 2022. "Novye iudeiskie epitafii II-IV vv. n.e. iz Kerchi" [New Jewish epitaphs of the 2nd-4th c. AD from Kerch]. Problemy istorii, filologii, kul'tury 4 (78), 115-146. — Context on the Panticapaean synagogue (mostly post-period). — PDF 404.
- Kashaev, S. V., and Kashovskaya, N. V., 2017. "Iudeiskie kamni i nadpisi Bospora." Khazarskii al'manakh 15. — Catalogue of Jewish stones and inscriptions of the Bosporus, with dating. — Not online.
- Collar, A., 2013. Religious Networks in the Roman Empire. Cambridge University Press, pp. 172-174, 186 (cited by Wikipedia "Pantikapaion"). — 1st-2nd c. CE inscriptions mentioning the Panticapaeum synagogue. — Paywalled.
- Bellen, H., 1965. "Die Aussage einer bosporanischen Freilassungsinschrift (CIRB 71) zum Problem der 'Gottfürchtigen'." Jahrbuch für Antike und Christentum 8, 171-176. — Reading/dating of CIRB 71. — Paywalled.
- Bekhter, A. P., 2025. "Irina Levinskaya: A New Look at the History of the Early Roman Bosporus." Petersburg Historical Journal 4 (48). — Summary of Levinskaya's evidence for Jews in the early Roman Bosporus from the 1st c. AD. — 503 error.
- [Article in Vestnik drevnei istorii], ras.jes.su/vdi/s032103910015302-5-1. — Bosporan Jewish manumissions/hypsistos bibliography. — 503 error.
