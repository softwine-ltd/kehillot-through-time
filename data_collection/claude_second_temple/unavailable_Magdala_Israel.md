# Sources not accessed: Magdala (Taricheae), Israel

- De Luca, S. and Leibner, U., 2019. "A monastery in Magdala (Taricheae)?" Journal of Roman Archaeology 32. https://journalofromanarchaeology.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/JRA32_16_Leibner.pdf — The full article reviews the town's history and Jewish population in the Early Roman period. — Only the first page is free; the rest is paywalled (Cambridge).
- De Luca, S. and Lena, A., 2015. "Magdala/Taricheae." In D. A. Fiensy and J. R. Strange (eds), Galilee in the Late Second Temple and Mishnaic Periods, vol. 2, Minneapolis, pp. 280-291. — A synthesis of the Franciscan excavations (Hasmonean foundation, urban plan, harbour, revolt damage); may give town size or population. — Not online (cited by De Luca and Leibner 2019).
- Bauckham, R. (ed.), 2018. Magdala of Galilee: A Jewish City in the Hellenistic and Roman Period. Waco: Baylor University Press. https://www.baylorpress.com/9781481302937/ — Twelve chapters on the excavations; includes M. H. Jensen, "Magdala/Taricheae and the Jewish Revolt" (pp. 269-286), plus Aviam's and Bauckham's chapters. May hold population and demographic estimates. — Only a publisher page and reviews were found; full text not online.
- De Luca, S., 2009. "La città ellenistico-romana di Magdala/Taricheae. Gli scavi del Magdala Project 2007 e 2008." Liber Annuus 59. https://www.brepolsonline.net/content/journals/10.1484/J.LA.1.102174 — The excavation report (Hasmonean tower and harbour, urban plan, revolt destruction). — 403 / paywalled (Brepols).
- Zapata-Meza, M., Garza Díaz-Barriga, A. and Sanz-Rincón, R., 2018. "The Magdala Archaeological Project (2010-2012): A Preliminary Report of the Excavations at Migdal." 'Atiqot 90, pp. 83-126. — Stratigraphy (Hellenistic to 67 CE), miqva'ot, domestic finds. — Abstract only (the anahuac.mx PDF has 2 pages); the full text was not accessed.
- Zapata-Meza, M. (ed.), 2025. Ancient Magdala: Archaeological Excavations (2010-2017), Volume I. Oxford: Archaeopress. https://www.archaeopress.com/Archaeopress/Products/9781803279831 — The final excavation publication; may give settlement extent. — Not fetched; book likely paywalled (only a sample PDF exists).
- Callegher, B., 2023. Coins and Economy in Magdala/Taricheae from the Hasmoneans to the Umayyad Period. Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht. — Full coin counts by period (Hasmonean, Jewish Revolt). — Book not online; known only through the BMCR review.
- Taylor, J. E., 2022. "Magdala's Mistaken Identity." Biblical Archaeology Review (Fall 2022). https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/article/magdalas-mistaken-identity — Argues that Taricheae is not Magdala, which matters for which Josephus figures apply to this site. — Paywalled (BAS Library).
- Kokkinos, N., 2010. "The location of Tarichaea: north or south of Tiberias?" Palestine Exploration Quarterly 142, pp. 7-23. — An alternative identification of Taricheae, which affects whether the Josephus figures belong to Magdala. — Paywalled (T&F); not attempted.
- Aviam, M., 1997. Archaeological summary of Magdala (cited by Leibner 2009: 220, "Aviam 1997: 399-400"; full reference not checked). — Describes a "small and well-planned town" in the Roman period; may give its size. — Not online.
- Adan-Bayewitz, D. and Aviam, M., 1997 (cited by Leibner 2009: 225 n. 96 as possible evidence for an organized Hasmonean settlement project in Galilee; full title not checked). — Context for the Judean settlement of Galilee under the Hasmoneans. — Not accessed; known only as a citation.
- Avshalom-Gorni, D. and Erlich, A., 2021/22. Report on the second Second Temple-period synagogue at Magdala (University of Haifa salvage excavation). — Would give the basis of the excavators' "several thousand residents" characterization. — No published report found online; only press coverage.
