- Seager, A. R. and Kraabel, A. T., 1983. "The Synagogue and the Jewish Community." In G. M. A. Hanfmann (ed.), Sardis from Prehistoric to Roman Times, Cambridge, Mass., pp. 168-190. — Standard account of the Sardis Jewish community, its origins and the Josephan decrees; may hold any scholarly size estimate. — Not online (book).
- Kraabel, A. T., 1968. Judaism in Western Asia Minor under the Roman Empire. Ph.D. dissertation, Harvard University. — Survey of Sardis Jewish evidence incl. Hellenistic/early Roman period. — Not online.
- Trebilco, P. R., 1991. Jewish Communities in Asia Minor. Cambridge University Press (SNTSMS 69), esp. pp. 37-54 on Sardis. — Detailed analysis of Ant. 14.235, 14.259-261, 16.171 and the Antiochus III settlement. — Paywalled / Google Books.
- Schalit, A., 1960. "The Letter of Antiochus III to Zeuxis regarding the Establishment of Jewish Military Colonies in Phrygia and Lydia." Jewish Quarterly Review 50, pp. 289-318. — Authenticity and date of the 2000-families letter, possible settlement at Sardis. — JSTOR paywall.
- Pucci Ben Zeev, M., 1998. Jewish Rights in the Roman World: The Greek and Roman Documents Quoted by Josephus Flavius. Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck. — Exact dating of the Lucius Antonius letter, Sardian decree and Norbanus Flaccus letter. — Not online (book).
- Magie, D., 1950. Roman Rule in Asia Minor. Princeton. — Cited by cojs.org for dating the Lucius Antonius letter to 49-47 BCE. — Not online / not checked.
- Mitchell, S., 1993. Anatolia: Land, Men, and Gods in Asia Minor, vol. 2, p. 33. Oxford. — Cited by Eilers for the "large and important Jewish population" of Sardis. — Not online (book).
- Robert, L., 1964. Nouvelles inscriptions de Sardes I. Paris. — Synagogue inscriptions (later period) and Hellenistic decree of Sardis. — Not online.
- Donner, H. and Röllig, W., 1962. Kanaanäische und aramäische Inschriften, no. 260. — Aramaic-Lydian bilingual naming Sardis SPRD (basis for the Sepharad identification). — Not online.
- Magness, J., 2005. "The Date of the Sardis Synagogue in Light of the Numismatic Evidence." American Journal of Archaeology 109.3, pp. 443-475. — Dating of synagogue (post-period), relevant to whether any building evidence predates 70 CE. — Paywalled (abstract only).
- Kroll, J. H., 2001. "The Greek Inscriptions of the Sardis Synagogue." Harvard Theological Review 94; Cross, F. M., 2002. "The Hebrew Inscriptions from Sardis." HTR 95. — Synagogue epigraphy (late Roman). — Paywalled.
- Bible Odyssey, "Sardis" article. https://www.bibleodyssey.org/articles/sardis/ — General history of Jews at Sardis. — 403 Forbidden.
- ANU Museum of the Jewish People database, "The Ancient Synagogue of Sardis Turkey." — Says Jewish settlement began in 3rd c. BCE; synagogue capacity c. 1000. — 403 Forbidden.
