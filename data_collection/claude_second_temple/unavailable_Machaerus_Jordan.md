# Unavailable sources: Machaerus, Jordan

- Vörös, Győző, 2016. "Bathing and Immersing in Machaerus: the Herodian Royal Bathhouse and the Four Ritual Purification Baths (Miqva'oth)." Liber Annuus 66, 321-349. DOI 10.1484/J.LA.4.2018014. — Dates and locations of the four mikva'ot. Per the search snippet, at least three were in use during the Baptist's imprisonment, one of them in the domestic quarter of the lower city. — Brepols 403/paywall; the RelBib record showed only a browser-verification page.
- Vörös, Győző, 2017. "Machaerus: A Palace-Fortress with Multiple Mikva'ot." Biblical Archaeology Review 43:4 (July/August 2017). https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/article/machaerus-a-palace-fortress-with-multiple-mikvaot/ — Full account of the mikva'ot, the lower city ("thousands of ordinary people") and its dating. — BAS Library paywall; only a one-sentence excerpt and a figure caption could be read.
- Vörös, Győző, 2013/2015/2019. Machaerus I: History, Archaeology and Architecture of the Fortified Herodian Royal Palace and City Overlooking the Dead Sea in Transjordan; Machaerus II; Machaerus III. Milan: Edizioni Terra Santa. — Final excavation reports: Hasmonean and Herodian phases, lower city, coins, stone vessels, mikva'ot, and possibly an estimate of the size of the lower city. — Not online.
