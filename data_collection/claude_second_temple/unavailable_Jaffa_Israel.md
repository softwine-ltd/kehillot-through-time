# Unavailable sources: Jaffa, Israel (Second Temple period)

- Arbel, Yoav, 2011. "The Hasmonean Conquest of Jaffa: Chronology and New Background Evidence." In M. Peilstöcker and A.A. Burke (eds.), The History and Archaeology of Jaffa 1 (Monumenta Archaeologica 26), Cotsen Institute of Archaeology Press, ch. 16. https://www.academia.edu/30940046 ; open-access volume at https://escholarship.org/uc/item/1v87091j — Why relevant: the abstract (seen only in search snippets) says Hellenistic Jaffa was predominantly pagan and the 142 BCE conquest made it predominantly Jewish, with Simon expelling the pagans; may hold archaeological evidence for the Judaization. — Why not accessed: academia.edu 403; the eScholarship PDF exceeds the fetch size limit and curl returned empty (202).
- Burke, Aaron A., 2011. "Early Jaffa: From the Bronze Age to the Persian Period." In The History and Archaeology of Jaffa 1, ch. 6. https://escholarship.org/uc/item/1v87091j — Why relevant: Persian-period (Sidonian) Jaffa, which bears on whether there was any Judean presence before the Hasmoneans. — Why not accessed: PDF too large to fetch.
- Jakoel, Eriola, (n.d.). [Article on Roman-period Jaffa]. Cathedra 159 (Yad Izhak Ben-Zvi). https://files.ybz.org.il/periodicals/Cathedra/159/2_Eriola_Jakoel_(Cat_159)_LR.pdf — Why relevant: Hebrew study of Jaffa and its population in the Roman period (Pompey's annexation, Josephus' evidence). — Why not accessed: 403 for both WebFetch and curl.
- Kasher, Aryeh, 1990. Jews and Hellenistic Cities in Eretz-Israel. Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck. — Why relevant: detailed treatment of Hasmonean conquest of Joppa, expulsion of gentiles and Jewish repopulation; reprisal context. — Why not accessed: book not online; review at ejournals.eu returned 503.
- Tsuf, Orit, 2024. "Stone Vessels." In M. Edrey, B. Gross and Z. Herzog, The Tel Aviv University Excavations in Ancient Jaffa, Vol. I (Salvage Excavation Reports 12), ch. 22 (French Hospital Compound); also Tsuf, chs. 6-7 (Persian and Hellenistic pottery and small finds, Tel Yafo); Johananoff, ch. 12 (coins). — Why relevant: chalk stone vessels and Hasmonean coins would be archaeological evidence of Jewish residents in Hellenistic-early Roman Jaffa. — Why not accessed: printed book; only the table of contents is online.
- Gendelman, Peter, 2025. "Pottery Vessels and Stone Artifacts from the Persian, Roman and Byzantine Periods at the French Hospital Compound, Yafo (Jaffa)." 'Atiqot, pp. 157-188. — Why relevant: possible early Roman stone vessels (Jewish marker). — Why not accessed: seen only as a search-result title, not found online.
- Rosenfeld, Ben-Zion, 2000. "Flavius Josephus and His Portrayal of the Coast (Paralia) of Contemporary Roman Palestine: Geography and Ideology." Jewish Quarterly Review 91(1): 143-183. doi:10.2307/1454789 — Why relevant: dates Simon's capture of Joppa (143 BCE) and covers the coastal cities' population in Josephus. — Why not accessed: JSTOR paywall.
- Goodman, M., J. Barton and J. Muddiman (eds.), The Apocrypha (Oxford Bible Commentary), p. 154. — Why relevant: cited by Wikipedia for the gradual replacement of Joppa's pagan population by Jews. — Why not accessed: not online.
- Kaplan, Jacob, 1981/2. "Evidence of the Trajanic period at Jaffa." Eretz-Israel 15: 413 ff. (Hebrew). — Why relevant: cited by Applebaum; mainly post-70 (Judah the agoranomos, 101-107 CE), but it sets the context for the first-century community. — Why not accessed: not online.
- Jones, A.H.M., 1971. The Cities of the Eastern Roman Provinces, 2nd ed., Oxford, pp. 273-276. — Why relevant: argues that Herod demoted Jaffa to a toparchy centre, which Applebaum takes to imply a sizable Jewish population. — Why not accessed: not online.
- Tolkowsky, Samuel, 1924 (Hebrew ed. 2001). The Gateway of Palestine: A History of Jaffa. — Why relevant: comprehensive history of Jaffa, including the Hasmonean expulsion and Jewish settlement. — Why not accessed: not found online in readable form.
- Schürer, E. (rev. Vermes, Millar, Goodman), The History of the Jewish People in the Age of Jesus Christ, vol. II, section on Joppa. — Why relevant: standard summary of Joppa's status and population. — Why not accessed: not online (Google Books blocked).
