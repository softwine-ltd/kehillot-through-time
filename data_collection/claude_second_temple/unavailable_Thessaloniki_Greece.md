# Unavailable sources: Thessaloniki, Greece (Second Temple period)

- Riesner, Rainer, 1998. *Paul's Early Period: Chronology, Mission Strategy, Theology.* Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, pp. 344-354 (pages as cited by Taylor 2002). — Taylor (HTS 2002) cites it for the "ambiguous inscriptional evidence for a Jewish population during the first half of the first century CE" in Thessalonica, and for the character of the community. — Not online (book; only a review found).
- Wanamaker, Charles A., 1990. *The Epistles to the Thessalonians* (NIGTC). Grand Rapids: Eerdmans, p. 4ff. — Cited for the view that "a significant Jewish community existed at Thessalonica" in Paul's time. — Not online.
- De Vos, Craig S., 1999. *Church and Community Conflicts: The Relationships of the Thessalonian, Corinthian, and Philippian Churches with Their Wider Civic Communities.* SBLDS 168. Atlanta: Scholars Press, pp. 129-130, 144-145. — Reconstructs the population of Thessalonica (overwhelmingly Macedonian, with immigrant minorities). — Not online.
- Noy, David, Alexander Panayotov and Hanswulf Bloedhorn, 2004. *Inscriptiones Judaicae Orientis I: Eastern Europe.* TSAJ 101. Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, pp. 95-105 (as cited by Ovadiah 2015; Thessalonica entries incl. Mac15). — The full corpus of Jewish and Samaritan inscriptions from Thessalonica with dates; it would confirm whether any predate 74 CE. — Paywalled / not online.
- Schiby, J., 1977, pp. 103-110 (as cited by Ovadiah 2015; title and journal not given in the citing source). — Edition of the bilingual Samaritan-Greek synagogue inscription. — Not online.
- Frey, J.-B., 1936. *Corpus Inscriptionum Iudaicarum* I, no. 693 (Abramios and Theodote epitaph, Thessalonica). — Gives the date of the earliest Jewish epitaph from the city (late 2nd c. CE per Ovadiah). — Not accessed online.
- Kloppenborg, John S. and Richard S. Ascough, 2011. *Greco-Roman Associations: Texts, Translations, and Commentary I.* BZNW 181. Berlin: De Gruyter, pp. 375-377 (no. 82). — Commentary on the M. Aurelius Iakob/Eutychios inscription that mentions "the synagogues" (3rd c. CE). — Paywalled.
- Levinskaya, Irina, 1996. *The Book of Acts in Its Diaspora Setting* (The Book of Acts in Its First Century Setting 5). Grand Rapids: Eerdmans. — Discusses Diaspora synagogues and god-fearers in Acts, possibly including Thessalonica. — Not online.
- [Author not identified], 2000. Article on the location of the synagogue where Paul preached in Thessaloniki. *Makedonika* 32. https://ejournals.epublishing.ekt.gr/index.php/makedonika/article/download/5726/5465 — Topography of the 1st-century synagogue and the Jewish presence. — 403 Forbidden.
- Nigdelis, P. M., [year unknown]. Article on the family of the Auli Avii, based on a bilingual dedicatory inscription, with a note on the Jewish community of Thessaloniki. *Tekmiria*. https://ejournals.epublishing.ekt.gr/index.php/tekmiria/article/download/2856/2609/5831 — May contain bibliography and evidence on the Roman-period Jewish community. — 403 Forbidden.
- [Author not identified]. Chapter on Hellenistic to Early Christian Thessalonica (Heidelberg University, Propylaeum). https://books.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/propylaeum/catalog/view/501/761/85538 — Mentions Jewish traders and artisans from Asia Minor and the Levant, known from Acts and from funerary inscriptions (footnotes 10-12). — Blocked by bot protection (Anubis access denied).
- Fujisawa, [first name unknown], 2017. "Religious Identity of Diaspora Samaritans in Thessaloniki." Poster, 15th International Congress of Greek and Latin Epigraphy, Vienna. — Dating and interpretation of the Samaritan inscription, and Jewish versus Samaritan synagogue location. — 404 Not Found.
- Nar, Alberto, 1997. *Keimeni epi aktis thalassis* (Thessaloniki: University Studio Press); and Nar, A., 1997. "Koinotiki organosi kai drastiriotita tis Evraikis Koinotitas Thessalonikis," in I. K. Hassiotis (ed.), *Tois Agathois Vasilevousa Thessaloniki*, p. 200. — Source of the 168 BCE and 140-143 BCE settlement hypotheses. — Not online.
- Nehama, Joseph. *Histoire des Israélites de Salonique* I (Greek translation 2000, p. 30). — Early settlement traditions. — Not online.
- Vakalopoulos, Apostolos, 1997. *Istoria tis Thessalonikis 316 BCE-1983*, p. 26. — Early Jewish settlement and the Strabo argument. — Not online.
