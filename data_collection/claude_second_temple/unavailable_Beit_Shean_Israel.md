# Unavailable sources: Beit Shean (Scythopolis), Israel

- Fuks, G., 1982. "The Jews of Hellenistic and Roman Scythopolis." Journal of Jewish Studies 33 (1-2), 407-416. — The main study of the Jewish community of Scythopolis from Judas Maccabee to the Talmudic period; may discuss the community's size and status before 66 CE. — Not online (paywall; known only from bibliographies and Grossmark 2023).
- Fuks, G., 1983. Scythopolis - a Greek City in Eretz Israel (Hebrew). Jerusalem: Yad Izhak Ben-Zvi, esp. pp. 90-97, 147-156. — Chapter on the Jewish community; note on ~30 Jewish ossuaries with Greek inscriptions (p. 149 n. 11); discussion of the expulsion of Jews after Pompey/Gabinius. — Not online.
- Kasher, A., 1988/1990. Jews and Hellenistic Cities in Eretz-Israel (TSAJ 21). Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, esp. pp. 78-81, 123-127, 259-261. — Analysis of 2 Macc 12:29-31, the Hasmonean conquest and the 66 CE massacre at Scythopolis. — Paywalled; only catalogue/review pages seen.
- Avi-Yonah, M., 1962. "Scythopolis." (pp. 45-62 as cited by Grossmark 2023; Israel Exploration Journal 12). — Argues the Jewish inhabitants were expelled to make room for a Hellenistic population after Pompey/Gabinius. — Not accessed (bibliographic entry only).
- Broshi, M., 1982. "The Credibility of Josephus." In U. Rappaport (ed.), Josephus Flavius, Historian of Eretz-Israel in the Hellenistic-Roman Period (Hebrew), p. 23. — Assessment that Josephus' 13,000 figure for Scythopolis is exaggerated. — Not online.
- Nieto Ibáñez, J. M., 1999. "The sacred grove of Scythopolis (Flavius Josephus, Bellum Iudaicum II 466-471)." Israel Exploration Journal 49 (3-4), 260-268. https://buleria.unileon.es/handle/10612/11712 — Study of the 66 CE massacre of the Jews of Scythopolis. — Repository returned 403.
- Lifshitz, B., 1978. "Scythopolis. L'histoire, les institutions et les cultes de la ville à l'époque hellénistique et impériale." ANRW II.8, 262-294. — City history including Hasmonean conquest and Jews. — Paywalled / not online.
- Fuks, G., 1981. "Antiochus Son of Phallion." Israel Exploration Journal 31 (3-4), 237-238. — Sarcophagus of 21/22 CE from Scythopolis; Encyclopaedia Judaica suggests the deceased was possibly a cousin of Herod the Great (would be a Herodian/Jewish burial). — Paywalled; only the IIP database record seen (which does not identify him as Jewish), so no row was written.
- Noam, V., 2003. Megillat Ta'anit: Versions, Interpretation, History (Hebrew), pp. 69, 196-197. — Commentary on the 15-16 Sivan entry (exile of the people of Beth Shean) and its date. — Not online.
- Mason, S., 2016. "Josephus's Autobiography (Life of Josephus)." In H. H. Chapman and Z. Rodgers (eds), A Companion to Josephus, pp. 70-73. — Differences between the War and Life accounts of the Scythopolis massacre. — Paywalled.
- Terrón Berrocal, S., 2025. "La presencia judía en las ciudades paganas de Palestina." Estudios bíblicos 83 (3), 413-457. https://produccioncientifica.usal.es/documentos/6a0610d49ad1cf5720dce61d — Survey of Jewish presence in pagan cities including Scythopolis. — Landing page only (bibliography), no full text.
