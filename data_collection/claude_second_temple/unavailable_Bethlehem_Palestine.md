# Unavailable sources: Bethlehem, Palestine (Second Temple period)

- Prag, K., 2000. "Bethlehem: a site assessment." Palestine Exploration Quarterly 132, 169-181. https://doi.org/10.1179/peq.2000.132.2.169 — Key reassessment of all archaeological data from Bethlehem, including Persian, Hellenistic and Roman remains and the extent of the town. — Paywalled (Taylor & Francis); only cited in reference lists.
- Prag, K., 2008. "R. W. Hamilton, D. C. Baramki and the Lower Aqueduct at Bethlehem." Palestine Exploration Quarterly 140:1, 27-38. — Excavated evidence of the Hasmonean/Herodian Lower Aqueduct running through Bethlehem; cited by the BAS article as evidence of an urban Second Temple town. — Paywalled; seen only as an endnote.
- Biblical Archaeology Society, "Not So Little Town of Bethlehem" (full article; author not shown on the open page). https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/article/not-so-little-town-of-bethlehem/ — Argues Bethlehem was a vibrant urban center in the Second Temple period; per search snippet, notes Josephus calls it a polis and argues it shrank to a village after Bar Kokhba. — Only the introduction is open; full text behind BAS Library subscription.
- Nigro, L. et al., 2015. Bethlehem survey/Khalet al-Jam'a article in Vicino Oriente (academia.edu item 22280922). https://www.academia.edu/22280922 — Notes Iron Age levels are disturbed by Hellenistic and Roman layers; reviews de Cree, Prag and Bagatti on the town's history. — 403 Forbidden.
- Nigro, L., Montanari, D., Ghayyada, M., Yasine, J., 2018. "Protecting and rehabilitating the archaeology of Bethlehem." Antiquity 92(365). — Read; contains Bronze/Iron Age necropolis data only, nothing for 586 BCE-70 CE. — Read but no Second Temple data (listed for completeness).
- Albright, W. F. (work not identified). Estimate of c. 300 inhabitants of Bethlehem at the time of Jesus' birth. — Total population estimate for the town. — Original not identified/not online; known only via Aleteia and knisja.mt.
- Murphy-O'Connor, J., The Holy Land: An Oxford Archaeological Guide (various editions), Bethlehem entry; and Pfann, S. (statements to AP, 1994). — Source of the 2,000-3,000 population estimate for 1st-c. BCE Bethlehem. — Book not online (Google Books blocked); estimate known only via AP report in Seattle Times.
- Bagatti, B., 1952. Gli antichi edifici sacri di Betlemme. Jerusalem: Franciscan Printing Press. — Roman-period topography of Bethlehem with complete list of ancient sources. — Not online.
- de Cree, F., (1980s-1990s). Studies on the history of Bethlehem (cited by Nigro et al.). — Historical reassessment of Bethlehem across periods. — Not online; citation only.
- Finkelstein, I., 2008/2010. "Archaeology and the List of Returnees in the Books of Ezra and Nehemiah" (PEQ 140) and related Revue Biblique article on Yehud's extent and demography; Zevit, Z., 2009, response (JHS). — Survey data for the sites on the returnees list (incl. Bethlehem) and the list's date; Persian-period settlement scale. — PEQ paywalled; not reached in time.
- Santa Clara University course page on Bethlehem dig sites (C. Murphy). https://webpages.scu.edu/ftp/cmurphy/courses/sctr027/dig-sites/06b-bethlehem.htm — Summary of Bethlehem archaeology by period. — 401 Unauthorized.
- Laub, K. (AP), Dec. 1994, The Virginian-Pilot copy. https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/VA-news/VA-Pilot/issues/1994/vp941215/12150643.htm — Same AP report on Pfann/Murphy-O'Connor estimate. — 410 Gone (read instead via Seattle Times archive).
