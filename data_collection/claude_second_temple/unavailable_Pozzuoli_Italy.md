# Unavailable sources: Pozzuoli (Puteoli), Italy

- Lacerenza, G., 1999. "L'iscrizione di Claudia Aster Hierosolymitana." In L. Cagni (ed.), Biblica et semitica. Studi in memoria di F. Vattioni, Napoli: Istituto Universitario Orientale, 303-313. — Full edition, reading and dating of the epitaph of a Jerusalem captive from Puteoli's territory (CIL X 1971; JIWE I 26). — Not online (cited by Lacerenza 2023 and Wikipedia).
- Noy, D., 1993. Jewish Inscriptions of Western Europe, I: Italy (Excluding the City of Rome), Spain and Gaul. Cambridge University Press (nos. 23, 26 for Puteoli). — Standard corpus with commentary and dating of the Puteoli Jewish inscriptions (gerusiarch Ti. Claudius Philippus; Claudia Aster). — Not online / paywalled.
- Frey, J.-B., 1936. Corpus Inscriptionum Judaicarum I (nos. 556, 561). — Older edition of the same inscriptions. — Not accessed (not found online as text).
- Applebaum, S., 1976. "The Social and Economic Status of the Jews in the Diaspora." In S. Safrai - M. Stern (eds.), The Jewish People in the First Century (CRINT I.2), 701-727 (esp. 706-707, 722). — Discusses the Puteoli Jews, the alabarch's agent, Aliturus. — Not online.
- Smallwood, E. M., 1961. Philonis Alexandrini Legatio ad Gaium. Leiden: Brill (comm. p. 256). — Commentary arguing the news of Gaius' order was brought to the envoys at Puteoli by Italian Jews. — Not online (quoted in Lacerenza 2004).
- Schwartz, D. R., 1990. Agrippa I: The Last King of Judaea. Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck (xv, 6-7, 50). — Disputes the existence of an agent of the alabarch Alexander at Puteoli. — Not online.
- Camodeca, G. - Caputo, P. - Giglio, M. (eds.), 2013. Materiali per lo studio storico-archeologico di Quarto Flegreo. Napoli: Università L'Orientale, 188-189. — Context of the gerusiarch inscription (CIL X 1893) findspot. — Not online.
- Giordano, C. - Kahn, I., 2001. The Jews in Pompeii, Herculaneum, Stabiae and in the Cities of Campania Felix (3rd ed., trans. W. F. Jashemski). Rome: Bardi. — Chapter II on the Puteoli epigraphic evidence. — Only the review (j-italy.org reprint of BMCR 2004.02.28) was read.
- Akroterion article (1994) on Jews in Campania / the Claudia Aster inscription, https://akroterion.journals.ac.za/pub/article/view/514/580 — Likely discusses the Claudia Aster epitaph as evidence for Jews in Campania. — Scanned PDF without a text layer; WebFetch could not read it.
- Judaism and Rome database (Heurist), record 289 "Dedication for a Jewish slave at Puteoli (CIL X, 1971)", https://heurist.huma-num.fr/heurist/judaism_and_rome/tpl/public-record/289 — Commentary on Claudia Aster and on the Puteoli Jewish community. — HTTP 503.
- den Hollander, W., 2014. Josephus, the Emperors, and the City of Rome. Leiden: Brill, 55-56. — Interpretation of Josephus' meeting with Aliturus at Puteoli. — Paywalled.
- Adinolfi, M., 1960. "San Paolo a Pozzuoli (Atti 28,13b-14a)." Rivista Biblica 8. — On the 'brethren' at Puteoli and the local Jewish background. — Not online.
