- Trebilco, P. R., 1991. Jewish Communities in Asia Minor. SNTS Monograph Series 69, Cambridge University Press. — Standard survey of Jewish communities in Asia Minor; likely discusses the Galba letter to Miletus and the theatre inscription. — Not online (book; only catalogue/review pages).
- Pucci Ben Zeev, M., 1998. Jewish Rights in the Roman World: The Greek and Roman Documents Quoted by Josephus Flavius. TSAJ 74, Mohr Siebeck. — Detailed commentary and dating of the Galba letter to Miletus (Ant. 14.244-246). — Not online (book; abstract/catalogue only).
- Ameling, W., 2004. Inscriptiones Judaicae Orientis II: Kleinasien. TSAJ 99, Mohr Siebeck. — Corpus of Jewish inscriptions of Asia Minor, including the Miletus theatre inscriptions and any earlier Milesian Jewish texts. — Paywalled / not online.
- Schürer, E., Geschichte des jüdischen Volkes im Zeitalter Jesu Christi, vol. III (4th ed.), pp. 80-81 n. 13 (and rev. Eng. ed. Vermes/Millar/Goodman, III.1, 1986). — Cited by the Brockhaus-Efron article for the Jews of Miletus and the Antiochus II citizenship question. — Not accessed (not found in fetchable form).
- Barclay, J. M. G., 1996. Jews in the Mediterranean Diaspora: From Alexander to Trajan (323 BCE-117 CE). T&T Clark / University of California Press. — Chapter on the province of Asia likely discusses the Milesian decree. — Not online (book).
- Berns, C., 2021. Chapter on the Miletus "synagogue" in S. Ovadya (ed.), Taşa Kazınan Yahudi Kimliği (Istanbul). — Latest assessment of the building's date and identification (late antique, outside period). — Not online.
- von Gerkan, A., 1921. "Eine Synagoge in Milet." Zeitschrift für die neutestamentliche Wissenschaft 20. — Original publication of the putative synagogue building (dated late 4th c. CE by the excavators, outside period). — Not online / not accessed.
