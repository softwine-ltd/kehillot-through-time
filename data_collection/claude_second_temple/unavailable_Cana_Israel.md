# Unavailable sources: Cana (Khirbet Qana), Israel

- Edwards, Douglas R., 2002. "Khirbet Qana: From Jewish Village to Christian Pilgrim Site." In J. H. Humphrey (ed.), The Roman and Byzantine Near East, vol. 3, Journal of Roman Archaeology Supplementary Series 49, pp. 101-132. — This is the main excavation synthesis and likely holds the Hellenistic and Early Roman phasing, the coin list, the miqvaot, the public building and the village size. — Not online (print JRA supplement).
- Eshel, Esther, 2002. "An Abecedary Inscription." In the same volume (Khirbet Qana section). https://cris.biu.ac.il/en/publications/an-abecedary-inscription/ — Publishes the Jewish-script abecedary ostracon and its dating. — Only a catalogue record online.
- Eshel, Esther and Douglas R. Edwards, 2004. "Language and Writing in Early Roman Galilee: Social Location of a Potter's Abecedary from Khirbet Qana." In D. R. Edwards (ed.), Religion and Society in Roman Palestine, Routledge, pp. 49-55. — Likely gives the ostracon's date and context and the Jewish identity of the village. — Paywalled or not online.
- Richardson, Peter, 2002. "What Has Cana to Do with Capernaum?" New Testament Studies 48.3, pp. 314-331. https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-testament-studies/article/abs/what-has-cana-to-do-with-capernaum/C66DAC600E10F048D1A64701459F1DF3 — Covers the village's character and size, and the identification. — Paywalled (abstract only).
- Richardson, Peter, 2006. "Khirbet Qana (and Other Villages) as a Context for Jesus." In J. H. Charlesworth (ed.), Jesus and Archaeology, Eerdmans, pp. 120-144. Reprinted in Richardson, Building Jewish in the Roman East (Brill/Baylor, 2004). — House typology, village plan, mikveh, possibly a household or population estimate. — Not online (Google Books blocked).
- McCollough, C. Thomas, 2015. "Searching for Cana: Where Jesus Turned Water into Wine." Biblical Archaeology Review 41.6, pp. 31-38. — Full article text: Hellenistic and Roman Jewish village evidence, Maccabean coins, miqvaot, synagogue. — BAS Library paywall (only captions and endnotes read).
- McCollough, C. Thomas, 2015. "Khirbet Qana." In D. A. Fiensy and J. R. Strange (eds.), Galilee in the Late Second Temple and Mishnaic Periods, vol. 2, Fortress Press. — An up-to-date excavation summary, possibly with size and population. — Not online (book).
- Edwards, Douglas R. (ed.), 2011. Khirbet Qana, a Galilean Village in Regional Perspective: Survey and Excavations 1997-2004. University of Puget Sound. — Survey data (site area) that could support a population estimate. — Not online.
- Rech, J. A., A. A. Fischer, D. R. Edwards and A. J. T. Jull, 2003. "Direct Dating of Plaster and Mortar Using AMS Radiocarbon: A Pilot Project from Khirbet Qana, Israel." Antiquity 77 (295), pp. 155-164. — Absolute dates for the Roman-period walls and floors, including the public building. — Paywalled (abstract only).
- CIIP editors (volume editors not checked), 2023. "XCI. Cana (mod. Ḥ. Qana)." Corpus Inscriptionum Iudaeae/Palaestinae, vol. 5/1: Galilaea and Northern Regions, De Gruyter, pp. 681-684. — Catalogue of Jewish inscriptions from the site with their dates. — Paywalled.
- Aviam, Mordechai, 2004. Jews, Pagans and Christians in the Galilee. University of Rochester Press, p. 56 (and passim). — Hasmonean and early Roman oil presses at Khirbet Qana, and the Hasmonean Judaization of Galilee. — Not online.
- Khirbet Qana Project annual excavation reports 1999-2008 and specialist coin reports (University of Puget Sound; IAA reports in Hebrew). — Coin counts (Hasmonean, revolt) and stratigraphy. — Access withdrawn temporarily (request via archaeology@pugetsound.edu).
- Jensen, Morten Hørning, 2006/2010. Herod Antipas in Galilee (WUNT II/215), Mohr Siebeck. This is the longer treatment cited in Biblica 93 (2012), n. 48. — Discussion of Cana's first-century stratum and its claimed destruction in the Jewish War. — Not online.
- Mason, Steve, 2001. Flavius Josephus: Translation and Commentary, vol. 9: Life of Josephus. Brill, p. 69. — Commentary on Life 86 (Cana) and its identification. — Paywalled.
